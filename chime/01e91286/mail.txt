Yuval Harari (TED Talk) proposes the reason we control earth is because we are able to put faith into stories (virtual artifacts like Religion, nations, money). This is compelling and rings true.

Suppose we state that as the most true property of humans and combines it with other insights to our character. What does this then tell us?

Our strongest asset is faith. We organize and strive because we can cooperate through an idea thats transcends the objective reality. We do this against reason and observed reality. In the light of this insight we can explain why we can go to war against those who has other faiths. Or even just the suspicion they are. We are threatened by other beliefs as strongly as if they would threaten us with weapons. Now this explains our irrationality. It is not so much that our rationality is flawed. It is that rationality is there to serve our beliefs. We are biased to rationalize (protect) our beliefs. Our beliefs must not be so easily overthrown, or they would not earn our trust. For beliefs to carry the strength to enable us to transcend obstacles in the objective world they must stand firm in the face of threats. As in religious beliefs that tells us to endure suffering also when no signs of better times can be seen. If our rational side then too easily could tell us to break out and fight for change, then our faith would not e our strong allie. No, we must use rationalize our beliefs for the faith to provide the strength to endure. To sacrifice our individual freedom for the good of society.

So, has time now come for the religion of the future to enable us to use faith to help us endure the true nature of reality and the flaws of our existence? What are the arguments that faith in the truth is the best way forward for us? Is the only way to know to design some religions of the future and see if the prevail in the selective forces of meme evolution?
