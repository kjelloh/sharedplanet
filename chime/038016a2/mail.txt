
1) Consider we can drive circular motion using magnet on a piston actuated with an electromagnet coil?

So, the basic idea is to drive a magnet inside an electromagnetic coil as a piston and transfer the motion to a drive wheel using a crank shaft.



We may consider to use three pistons in a three-phase configuration to ensure we can control the rotation direction and also have a reasonably even torque over the cycle.

2) Consider the wikipedia article on "Reciprocating electric motor" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reciprocating_electric_motor)?

	==> It seems the main disadvantage of this design is that the magnetic field strength drops with distance causing an uneven and ineffective drive force over the cycle?
