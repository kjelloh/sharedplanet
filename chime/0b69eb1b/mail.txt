I thought the next step in creating my super tiny apartment makerspace - was to create a better floor and organisation for it.
Now, I am still far away - from having a space where I can cut wood - using my table saw. - Unshielded - It will just create to much dust!
But by getting the flooring done - then at least - I have a base to start building my - “indoor shed”.
Lets see how it goes.
The first thing I need to do - is to temporarily fill this hole in the floor - with something that can support some weight. 
I imagine - Some currently unused wood have to suffice!
So, * whistle *
* stop motion of wood getting into the floor *
Now - for the floor - I will use thin white laminated boards.
They where reasonable cheap - seem sturdy - and if white flooring is important for Elon Musk factories - it should be a good choice for me - eh?
I needed to measure and cut the first one - to make it fit tightly between the restricting walls.
On the floor - I placed the board I had used as my workbench top.
And with a 45 by 45 mm beam (a 2 by 2) as a spacer…
...
...I was able to make the cuts with my Jigsaw.
And after having made some adjustment…
to make it fit…
I cleaned up the mess…
- And cleared the area for the new flooring.
...
I started by laying out some protecting plastic film - to protect my apartment floor.
Then I put the laminate sheets in place.
…making sure the plastic film came out on the top.
I now realised I could try having a wood storage for my sheet wood in the makerspace area instead of having it on my wood storage cart.
The 2.2 by 1.2 meter sheets - just did not fit were well when placed on the cart.
I think this works better!
Now I could move all my tools back - into the makerspace area.
Note that I have mounted the legs onto my table saw stand.
… hoping this will enable me to begin using it quicker - than if I first was to construct and build some home made fancy integrated solution.
Long story short - I think this is a significant improvement!
Now I can start planning how to protect my apartment from dust produced by my power tools - especially my table saw.
More about that in later episodes.
So, there you have it. Another step in bootstrapping my tiny apartment indoor wood shop and maker space.
