Any system that is explained by cause-and-effect will always encounter border problems. What caused the system to come into existence (original cause) that is not an effect? And what are the original causes that continues to cause all the ”new” causes in the system?

1) What caused the universe to come into existence? And where do all causes in the universe originate if not from ”outside” when it was created?
2) What caused life to come into existence? And what causes life to continue to exist and not fall back into lower entropy?
3) What caused our consciousness to come into existence? And what make our consciousness to go away (as when we sleep or drift into non-thinking) and come back again?
4) How did God come into existence if he exist? And if he exist, why do he keep the world around?
5) What about the Mathematical universe that Max Tegmark talks about. Does it even adress the question of ”what caused it”? Is not a mathematical universe just about how to model the effects when we know a cause? But how can such a universe adress the question of the ”original cause”?
6) Is cause-and-effect actually in itself an illusion? That would dove the problem? Or rather, we break the cause-and-effect chain but we still have causes. Where do they keep coming from? Is this then reduced to the question ”why is there something rather than nothing”?
