We have introduced the term Dividual as being the ”individual” created (raised) to interact with the situation at hand.

1) This is not the Dividual talked about in historic sense. In http://p2pfoundation.net/Dividual this dividual is simply different divisions of the individual. One seems to still keep the notion that we have a core but the dividuals are different aspects of that individual (as defined by stored data and roles given different access to the virtual world).  
2) Dividualism https://soclibfilosofi.wordpress.com/2014/10/19/dividualism-contra-individualism/ seems to be more on track although they do not talk about the core-less aspect of our existence.  
3) Our Dividual is the unique conscious our brain creates for us to interact with the current situation. It may have traits totally unrelated to any of the other dividuals we can become in other situations. The dividual is created for us based on the truths (ideas or memes) we have adopted in relation to the situation the dividual is called to relate with. The dividual is thus an emergence and there is no ”core” individual. The ”core” consciousness does not exist. Consciousness only exists when created. At all other times it is not there.

Consider to define this view of the Dividual and compare it to previous definitions. Also consider to use another term if Dividual in not a good one. The aspect of the consciousness Emergence may call for a term carrying the label ”synthesis” or ”Emergence”? On the other hand we are so use to the term Individual as ”the core conscious us” that we should be tempted to go for ”Dividual” as this new concept?
