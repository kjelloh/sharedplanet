	==> Consider how this can also be modeled as that Rationality is at its core the ability to hide and exclude (and therein lays both its power and its flaws)?

We can consider only the Occam's Razor to see how important and central it is in the rational model of making sense to focus on what to exclude. The whole adventure of rational thinking begins with being really picky on what to allow in into the process of reasoning. But this ability to exclude also can fall prey to not being able to realise that we can’t rationally decide what so see, what to include. It seems the way we get the things to reason about is from our unconscious and emotionally framed “machinery” that picks out and exposes things that exist. The rational process is NOT the observer nor the revealer of facts. It is the servant that provides both higher order but also second order opinions on what is true about the things already presented as identified to exist by our observer self, the what-to-consider self.

It seems we can make a case that this also rimes well with research that seems to suggest that our cosncsiouss self is NOT the part of us that makes decisions. But it is the part that can veto decisions taken by our unconscious and what-to-include self. We can, it seems, explain this as what free will is about. We can’t willingly decide what to see or what to care about. But we can veto against what we feel an urge to do. Our conscious rational self, in its bubble if being excluded from our unconscious self, is aware of a self-evident reality whilst being at the same time totally unaware this reality is a ready-to-consume one produced, packaged and delivered by hidden “machinery” between us and the reality out there.

I can’t resist to speculate that the entity we refer to as “God” is to at least a large degree a model of this unseen producer-of-reality inside ourselves that we need to be able to negotiate with, to take guidance from and to understand how to interpret the messages from. And although I am still, to the degree I can label myself in any meaningful way, an Atheist. I can’t rule out the possibility that because evolution seems to be a process where we internalise a model of the reality we interact with so that our act-in-faith to that model proves to be recurse-able; A model that itself is encoded and represented in whatever reality is made up of (our interior also exist in reality); Then in whatever form there is something we can refer to as “God” out there, it could very well also seep into our internalised machinery and thus exist in the same mysterious way our consciousness exists. Its all very foggy and incomprehensible in so many ways. But whatever is in our unconscious ready-to-digest, self-evident-creating reality-producer, in a model of it the concept of “God” so far seems to go free from Occam’s razor.
