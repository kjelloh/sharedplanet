Thoughts to follow up on:

a) Implication on our inability to experience truth. Our remembered happiness is not very correlated to how the remembered experience really was! Our memory does not take into account the time for example. So if an experience is to a large extent a happy one but it ends in an unhappy feeling, then our remembered happiness will be very much lower than if the opposite was true. So, yet another example of how groundless our records of life is and how well equipped we seem to be to adopt fitting lies unseated of the actual truth about matters in our lives.  
b) Behavioral Economics sounds really interesting! This could be touching on the thoughts about how economic systems relate to the nature of us as humans?
