Consider to put down some basic criteria (motivations) to build a model of society 2.0.

1. All human actions must be viewed as actions within the society. What I do affects others. What others do affects me. Humanity now fills and overruns the boundaries defined by this planet production of renewable and fixed resources. We can no longer argue that sub-societies (e.g. countries, cooperations or families) may act as if dis-engaded from other sub-societies. We share this planet and must thus share resources, space and energy. We can’t allow cooperations to act as if disengaged from society. Cooperations acting on a market economy, protection of owners rights and freedom for customers to by goods and services must be viewed as part of society and allowing or regulating these actions must be based on evaluating the common good for society as a whole. If the incitements promote actions that lead to unsustainable processes, growing inequality, unhappiness (however we define it), belittlement of human existence, failure to adress our insatiability and mortality, then these incitements are not good for society and should be replaced.
2. We basically act irrational. We must build a society that acknowledge the flaws of the human nature. Basically we are driven by needs communicated through feeling with ability for reflection. We must acknowledge that we tend to bend reality to fit our view of the world. Thus we must motivate the rules of society using both emotional and rational claims.
3. Society emerges from our individual actions based on basic believes rooted in religious like doctrines. Our society is self organizing in the same way as an ant or bee society. Each individual acts based on unconscious drives triggered by other individuals actions and ideas. Basically each human entity is a vessel for genes and a platform for ideas. Genes and ideas (memes) compete for survival using our brains and bodies as tools. With the introduction of a global network of communication (starting with the invention of written language, then the printing press and now internet) the meme evolution is accelerating. As human beings we should try to use our consciousness to design incitements that makes the emergent society be one where we as dividuals experience the optimal possible life. The human society must acknowledge the self organizing force of society (the ant society mechanism) adding the goal that each ”ant” must also experience enough freedom, tools and possibilities to experience a good life for themselves, not only as ”slaves” to the society.
4. Society should trigger our good qualities and minimize triggering of our ”bad” ones. We know we have in ourselves the ability to misstrust strangers, be greedy and to apply violence and even war when the conditions for this occurs. But we also excel among all living creatures in the ability to bond with, or at least accept pretense of, total strangers. We are able to feel good about sharing with others, to promote justice and cooperate to bring new and better ideas to existence. Knowing about the flaws of our human condition enables us to design a society that uses optimize the forces of our good sides and minimize triggering of our ”bad” sides.
5. Society must acknowledge change. We can no longer pretend the structure and ideas that lives in the society can become fixed. Change is the only constant and we must use all our abilities as humans to accept and make it to our advantage.
6. We must know that we believe and stop believing that we know. Although our brain has shown abilities to ”understand” a remarkable amount about what seems to be true and false about reality, we must still accept that each individual must still trust knowledge discovered by others. Further, our brains will forever be restricted in the amount of knowledge they are capable of storing and the accuracy of that storage. We must understand that we are able to build knowledge about reality and store in databases that are restricted in size only by the size of the universe, but our brains does not have that capacity. Thus, our ability to take advantage of knowledge and ideas to create richer and more sustainable lives for us all relies to a large extent on our ability to trust this knowledge whilst at the same time challenge it. The society must continuously balance between blind trust that risk society to gullibly accept anything and blind distrust that hinders us to make progress. A core idea of the society must thus be to keep searching and keep enhancing methods to search in the best way currently known.
7. The society must allow different ways of living, different ideas of what a good life is. In the same way society must be based on some basic core objectives that all may agree on. Society must accept that every argument about what a good society is and why it is so is groundless. Society must find the best groundless ground to build on whilst allowing the groundless ground to shift as the result of new knowledge or new ideas. The groundlessness of society comes from the groundlessness of our existence. Our current knowledge about our own flaws, especially our current failure to experience reality as-it-is and the freedom we have to choose the purpose of life, tells us that the argument for the goals of the society are groundless and must be chosen as axioms. We must choose if we like a society that accepts a good life for an elite whilst sacrificing lives of the rest. We must choose if we want other life forms to co-exist with us or if we want to sacrifice nature as we know it to gain other advantages. We must choose if the purpose of life is to excel in material experiential richness. We must accept the vertigo of this groundlessness and still select a virtual ground to build society on.
8. We must always be open to re-invent society whenever needed for whatever reason that arises from progress. We should select a smooth ever going peaceful revolution opposed to a series of painful violent overthrowing violent ones.
