Dawking and the Atheist movement is on the right track but non believing is just the first step.

The next step must be that we actually all believe. We can’t know, we must believe.

But what we actually mean with not-beleiving and trust reason is that we must continuously search for the currently best fitting belief. We may never know or come to the final truth (for one just based on the restriction of the human brain that can hold only finite amount of information) but we must keep searching it.

The atheist movement should thus be encourage to change the motto to one that actually fits this fact about the human brain restricted ability to know anything.

What they actual mean may something along the lines - know you believe. Design you beliefs to fit you the best.

Maybe not so catchy, but may allow discussion of what ”best for you” means. And at least state that ”blind belief” is equal to ”donut reason”.
