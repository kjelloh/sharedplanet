One way of addressing the problem that voters can’t have the sufficient insight in every question of action is to vote not about actions but about goals and priorities. Society, one would at least argue, would have to be able to delegate decision about action to those that arguably knows ”what is the best way”. And this would be a way to allow this.

If the election was about the goals and ideology that shall guide the society, then the representatives in power would be more free to select the actions that best drives society to those goals.

Now, this may not be true in all cases? There is likely actions that are preferred and others that must be avoided. Question is, if these restrictions should be stated into ideology or if ranges of allowed actions should also be voted about?
