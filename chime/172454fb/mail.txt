Often the divids we come to be in the face of situations are in fact agent for the ”truth” we hold about that situation. 

1) If we believe immigrants are violent and dangerous we become rasist if an immigrant commits a crime. If we believe everyone are greedy egoists we become indignant when people in power earns a lot of money.

2) One divid may be forced to defend the actions of another as when a CEO of an oil company is blamed for polluting the environments. The CEO may very well understand that the environment shall be protected but in the truth of the company goals the CEO divid does not think so. If the CEO had worked at greenpeace the same person would have been indignant about the very same action (same person, other truth, other divid).

We should pay more attention to the ”truths” we accept in society and what agents for these truth end up doing. When we blame people for doing bad things we need to ask ourselves what truth that divid is an agent for. This enables us to understand that it may not be people we shall blame but inly one divid of that person and the truth that divid is defending and thus becomes the victim of.
