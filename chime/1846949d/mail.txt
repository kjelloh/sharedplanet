I have painfully dragged myself closer and closer to actually be able to start painting my walls, but I have yet to begin and succeed to do it.

1) Consider to "spill the beans" and try to observe myself introspectively to try and overcome my fear of painting the walls and get the fresh to be able to sell my apartment?

There's no other way to put it. I am close to terrified to paint my walls! And the anxiety I feel when pushing forward to get this paint job done, is astonishing.

I act as if I am going to do a bungy jump. Instead what I try to do is, buy some paint and a brush, and then just open the can and apply the paint to the wall. How hard can it be?

Still, I get confused and back off over and over again. I am afraid I will buy the wrong paint. I am afraid I will buy the wrong colour. I am afraid I will be unable to paint "inside the lines", that is against the roof, the floor and side walls that are not to be painted. I am afraid my wall papers will buckle, or worse fall off when they absorb the water based paint. I am simply afraid I will fail and end up messing everything up! So that I am at a worse place then when I started.

To make matters worse I don't want to wash my tools in the sink and pollute the sewages. Although the paint is water based it is still poisonous.  But this is not how anyone is dealing with water based paint. You can't buy any tools or equipment to rinse your brush from paint into some closed container for waste water. Also, I want to buy only the tools I need and be able to re-use them in the future. So I do not want disposable tools. And good luck getting good advice from any store about what tools to buy that will get the job done and last a long time. That is just not how the home owner are supposed to do things. They are supposed to buy all these prescribed set of tools and chemicals , do the job as instructed and then just throw the tools and left overs away. And that is the last thing I want to do!

So yes, I am making this more difficult than I have to. But I have strong reasons for doing it. I have found my drive has always been to find the "best" way of doing things. And my "best" seems to be about wasting the least amount of resources and time to ge the job done. Especially if I also have to spend money. I just feel ill to spend hard earned money, on products that don't enable me to do the job the way I imagine it to be most cost effective, and at the same time seems designed to be used only once. And it does not help how much I try to tell myself that I should just "go with the flow", to get the job done, and then be able to focus on more important things. But I'm starting to imagine that I actually feel that solving a problem and doing a job "the right way" is, in fact, the most important thing for me! This is what I find most meaningful. So then, going with the flow, just makes me sad and discontent with my life.

So how am I to push through? How can I identify what I need to do, to get the wall painted, while not doing it in ways that makes me feel bad? How do I walk the thin line between procrastinating and planning in absurdum, and cave in and violate my ideals on how an effective and meaningful wall paint job, should be performed? Argh... this drives me crazy!

What if I try top face my worst fears first by actually start actualising some wall painting operations. The theory being that I can benefit from some self inflicted KBT treatment? It is reasonable, is it not, that there is no way around to actually start interacting with reality to build up some implicit learning and thus become less anxious about the prospect of screwing things up? I should be able to break this problem down into smaller pieces of operations and train them to gain confidence to do the whole wall?

Fear number one; I fear I will spill paint when opening the can and pour paint into the hand held container. So I can train that! I can open the can, pour some paint into the hand held cup. Then I can simply pour it back into the paint can and then close the can and clean the cup? Let's do it!
