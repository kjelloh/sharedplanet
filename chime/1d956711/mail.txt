One way of introducing an economy that values sustainability and ”invaluable” aspects of our resources could be to create electronic money with a value that changes with the ”real” cost of the transacted item. We can call this the Psychic-paper mechanism as in the Psychic-paper used in the TV series of Dr Who. The paper shows what is needed to the one it is shown to. In that respect the paper adapts its properties to the context. A Psychic-paper money adapts its value to the participants and the environment of the transaction in which it is involved.

1) The money may inherit value properties depending on how it was earned or provided. In this way each item of money can carry properties that define for what at can be used and to what value depending on the transacted item. For example, some money items may be used to buy items that are expensive to produce (high and not so sustainable impact on shared resources) whilst other keep their value only in ”normal” transactions.
2) The money may adapt its value depending for what it is used. Its value may rise if used to purchase preferable items (items produced according to hight standards regarding sustainability) or decay when used for items expensive to produce (low standards according to sustainability).
3) The money may even carry a history of several transactions where it has been used and adjust its value accordingly. One may for example have the value decay with time and number of transactions to allow incitements to circulate money (ensure wealth spread) but reduce life time (prohibit unproductive finance transactions).

Consider to program such a money as a proof-of-concept?

Note: This money must carry an incitement for use. Such an incitement will counterweight the urge to use other currencies that holds better value in each transaction. If the incitement of using the money is strong enough the cost of not getting the supply-demand value that may for unwanted items be higher (i.e. the urge to trade items for which the shard planet currency would hold a low value but would be highly sought for based on demand only).
