The evangelists of modernism and the importance to apply reason as the tool to create progress of humanity seems to fail to ground their cause by actually study the human nature and behavior. Can it really be true that all actions we do not like comes from humans not applying due reasoning? Or is it in fact more reasonable to think that all humans apply reasoning in the same way, just based on different needs? The explanation to why we see unreasonable actions is because we are blind to their reasons?

1) At least, it seem if so many apply reasoning in the ”wrong way” that to have any chance to actually creating a change we need to provide another idea of how to approach reality that actually a majority of humanity can adopt? If we agree that the goal is to minimize suffering and maximize pleasure in life we can start to collaborate to find the best way of enhancing life and society in that direction.
2) If the correct way of ”reasoning” is required to actually contribute and be a part of a better society, the we inherently build into such a society that those incapable or not wanting to adopt this type of thinking will be discriminated? We can’t build a society where only the ”smart” are able to collaborate and thrive. We need the most inclusive society possible and that includes also those with ”low IQ” and apostofates.
3) The idea on how to improve human lives and the conditions of humanity met be based on at least some understanding on how humans and humanity actually ”works”. The idea can’t be one where a majority of humans is required to radically change and adopt a doctrine they have not already.
4) We need to embrace the fact that all that persist to behave ”unreasonably” is in fact as reasonable as one can expect. And so, from that point of view, why do they seem to act in a way that is not beneficial to humanity or even to themselves. Could it be that in fact they are? We just don’t like how the actions threatens our own way of life? And if so, what idea for a society can we provide that actually enables us to share this planet with a minimum of suffering and a maximum of pleasure for all life?
