When Greta Thunberg addresses the UN with the words "How Dare You" with the subtext that those "in power" has stood by and done nothing to the environmental Issues one wonders to what degree those "in power" is in fact powerless?

The idea here is that we look at institutions, elected representatives and those with "money" as having power but to what degree is that actually true? For one , elected representatives has been put in position by mechanisms based on elections and competition and as such arguably can't be expected to suddenly act to adress problems they where not aiming to adress in the first place.

In fact, to get the society we want we should adress the general public to unite around a vision of the future that enables their action to result in a society self-organising to reach such goals?

Not to mention that it is questionable what "power" UN actually have to change anything when what actually takes place through the action of each and every individual human is driven by totally other forces than those dictated by UN, or any government. True, UN and governments can adjust taxes, put laws and regulations in place. But to actually make each human change their behaviour it seems reasonable we need to play on more basic human drives than restrictions and penalties?
