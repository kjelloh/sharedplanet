> *The other day I had to but a tube and a bottle on the rim of my sink in the bathroom. And because there was actually not room for both the tube and bottle at the same time I pushed the bottle to fall off and down into the sink. I had simply failed to accept the fact that the tube would not fit together with the bottle on the rim at the same time. But something in me just resist to accept the fact. I have discovered I can interact with reality a little better if I consciously remind myself to really study reality and then adapt to it. In this case just accept I have to do the extra work to place the tube in relation to the bottle so that none of them falls off the rim of the sink.*

> *When I have to pick up stuff to move them back to where they belong I often tries to bring everything along at the same time failing to realise that with my hand full I will be unable to say open a cupboard or extend a drawer. So I fail to accept the reality of gravity and the restriction of me having only two hands. And to effectively move stuff from A to B I have to take just enough with me to have at least one hand free to operate any doors or drawers required to successfully get the stuff to where it should be.*

> *Many things I have are stored in some kind of "pile" because I fail to accept that for stuff to have home I may need to buy or make some form of storage suited to let me have easy access to the stuff. What I fail to accept, or even become consciously aware about, it that I need to either take some stuff out of the cupboard, or to buy or make some form of "brush holder" that can co-exist with the stuff in the cupboard and at the same time co-exist there with all the other stuff.*
