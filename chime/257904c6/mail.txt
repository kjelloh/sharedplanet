A Todo-app would be much more beneficial in interacting with the user to guide where to place the focus if the Todos can be ordered into graphs showing what Todos that ”matters the most” and how the hierarchy of Todos may look like.

1) The ”Matters the most” function may change depending on the current drives of the user. Thus different relations ”wight of impact” on the Todos must be selectable. The graph may be show one set of nodes as the most influential ones if the user is at work or at home. Or it may look differently if the user is in the mood to invent or in the mood to relax or "have fun”.
2) The App should allow each Todo to be tagged with some properties that makes sense from the point of the different evaluation functions. To be able to relate the Todos to their ability to provide ”Survival”, ”efficiency” or ”hygiene” or the feeling of ”fun”, ”innovation” or ”relaxation” they must carry intrinsic properties with relation to those aspects. The relations between Todos must then also define what weight the relation has in relation to the intrinsic properties. This may be viewed as a way to define a ”more important than” relation between Todos as defined by ”where” the user is geographically, temporarily and emotionally.
3) The divid-model may have teeth to define this ability of the Todo-app? If we explain the aspect of where the user is, in what mood the user is and where in date and time the user is as different dividuals we are able to call the wanted mechanism to be divid-based. The app shows the appropriate Todo-graph best adapted to the traits of the targeted divid!
