If one thinks of it, a vote is actually a currency by which one may bid on a representative of power. The vote currency us non-transferable and unreadable to be used only once and only in one market (the elections).

Now, if we define electronic money that carries properties about its creation, transaction history and application, one may easily see a vote to be a special breed of this money. You can own only one and you can bid only once and only on representatives of government.

Now, if we take this step we may go on and allow a market of power to evolve. We may allow the vote to be transferable (used by a trusted agent). Different types of votes may be handed out for different aspects if power. All kinds of participative voting may be created by tuning the properties of this vote currency and market. The market may be continuous alive (to allow continuous voting in ongoing matters). One may even think about financing projects one votes for by combining the vote money with other type of money for which trade and goods may be bought?
