If there is a function that evaluates how good the current state of society is regarding its many parameters, then we could model this as a field in a multidimensional space (the dimensions being the uncoupled properties of society we choose to evaluate.

There are many problems with such a model.

1) It is not sure how we may invariant dimensions of the model space. How do we pick invariant properties of our society that has an impact of the society greatness-state?  
2) If this space and goodness-function exists, then the problem may be reduced to an optimization problem with all the known problems of finding the best of distributed local optimum points. As known from optimization problems these may be hard to find as their location are not known and field variation may provide no clues to where they are. Also, if we find a new better state of society, we may be unable to iterate ourselves there because the intermediate states (the path) are very unwanted states indeed!  
3) The relational function of society ”greatness” (i.e. the chosen Better-than function) may not be transitive! This would mean the model breaks down as there will be no way to calculate the greatness-field in the multi-dimensional space! The relation between points in this space must be transitive, otherwise the point location has no meaning. For example, if the transformation of position in this space enables us to go further and further away from the origin but we still end up in the center, this breaks down the meaning of position. Intransitivity of location transformation will create loop-holes in the space making the search for the optimal location impossible!
