2) Consider what I can learn and how I should think about my tendency to start but not finish projects?

> *I still think I would appreciate to actually finish my projects. I mean it is a possibility that I should just enjoy the planning and research and not be so concerned with actually making things in the real world? But somehow that does not make sense. As I see it there should be at least two things that are true about human endeavours. For one the knowledge should be shared so that what is potentially of value can be applied by others in their projects. Secondly one can not escape the fact that the value of anything can not be estimated until it is put to the test of applying it in action in the real world so that it actually affects other living things. If the stuff I do only affects my relation to dead things it still has value to me but I think my satisfaction is only triggered to its maximum potential if the ideas I have or the things I make has some positive impact on the reality of other humans or other living creatures?*
