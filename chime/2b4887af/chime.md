By defining Deep Richness and Wealth as all resources and conditions that defines a truly rich life one opens up for incitements to better distribute monetary wealth. One may realize that to become Deep Rich one has to spend monetary capital. To maximize ones Deep Capital one has to transact monetary capital to invest in goods and services with higher Deep Values.

1) At one level this is no more strange than to buy a trip to some sunny vacation resort. One decides one is more Deeply Rich from the experience of the trip than from say saving the money in a back account.  
2) At a deeper level a discussion of what Deep Richness is Shared Planet Citizens will decide to transact more monetary capital in return for higher Deep Wealth. And these transacted monetary capital may thus be circulated to enable monetary poor to raise from monetary poverty.  
3) On a society level the monetary regulations and spending by government may be guided to produce a higher level of Deep Wealth both for society as a whole but also with regards to the services provided by society. By trying to maximize the Deep Value from the monetary economy a larger part of the monetary capital may be distributed more fairly in society. This will be incitements to level out the differences in monetary wealth between members of the society.
