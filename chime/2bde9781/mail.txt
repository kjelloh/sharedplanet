1) Digital Receipts require a Digital Identity of the customer to send the receipt to.

	==> There is currently no way to use a single Digital Identity for Digital Services (each service defines a new Digital Identity and attach to the person)
	==> Existing Digital Receipt habitats include defining a Digital Identity of the user within that habitat.
	==> Different Digital Receipt habitats may be unable to share Digital Receipts.  

2) Compare Digital Receipt and Physical Receipt traits and application

	==> Physical Receipts can be handed to the Physical Customer at the Point of Purchase.
	==> All existing Point Of Sales are able to produce a physical receipt.
	==> All aspects of storing and processing physical receipts together with rules and laws for doing so is well established and global.
	==> Corresponding aspects for Digital Receipts are not in place.
