1) The most beneficial truth covers both religious and scientific truths.  
2) Science looks for the most beneficial truths to understand nature and enable us to predict what technology that may work to provide for our needs.  
3) Religion look for truths from which we may benefit by enabling us to collaborate in large numbers and endure hardship and sorrows.
