An alternative to individualism could be to instead embrace the possibility that in interaction between two humans we each create a divid best suited to monitor and take part in that interaction.

This view can be interesting and have many implications

1) It embraces the core-less view of ourselves. We do not carry a core individual within. Instead we carry genes, memes and memories in a physical body that shows as traits in the way we relate to events and how we choose act.
2) Finding one-self is of no use. We should instead embrace our dividual nature and accept all the ways it may manifest depending on the situation at hand. This also enables us to keep the future open when it comes to who we may be.
3) Trusting that we raise as JIT-created divids to meet a situation enables us to embrace super-curiosity. Act-in-faith then enables us to meet new situations and enjoy the divid we become rather than be frightened that we turn out to be a person we do not recognize! We can more truly accept and experience who we become and learn from that. We do not have to struggle with our self-image being challenged. It can’t be challenged. Who we are will always be a mystery but we may through experience learn what divids we may expect to turn up in different situations.
4) We should then argue that society should be constructed in such a way that the divids we like to populate it with are then ones that emerges. For example, to reduce racism we should construct society to not foster interactions that make rasist divids spring to life? Hm, kind of lame. But if we think about this some more maybe we can define what this can actually mean?
5) The divid created for the occasion may be seen as an emergence form gene and meme rules we carry? This could explain why we tend to not take in facts that shows that we may be wrong? For memes and divids to survive the must have at least some threshold before they change. Otherwise they will not get the chance to challenge the ”truth” and possibly prevail and showing that the ”truth” was not as good as was claimed.
