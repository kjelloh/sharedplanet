In a society where we are supposed to find and cherish our true individual self it can’t be a surprise when some come to require the same approach to defining groups of people. Nationalism is in that sense nothing more than a try to define the true core traits of a society, e.g. a nation. How can we say that Individualism is a virtue but nationalism is not?

1) The individual is fought integrity to stand up for the persons right to her core traits. The Nationalist does the same for the cultural core identity. Other cultures (core traits) are threats and a nation must apply its integrity to stand up to its right to its core traits.
2) Individualism is about competing to earn the rights to progress and success. The Nationalist argues the same goes for nations. A nation earns the right to wealth and happiness by how is succeeds in a competitive world. You have to stand up for yourself (individual or nation) because none else will.
3) Success is measured in monetary values and freedom from obeying the rules of others. Money is the way to freedom and the more money you have, the freer you are to express yourself as the true individual you are. The same thus goes for nations. A Nations success is measured in how free it is to not obeying the rules of other nations and in how much wealth it has in monetary values.
4) For the individual success is measured in the amount others envy her for what she have and does. Being seen and envied by many is for the individual a sign of richness. Thus a successful nation is one that are seen and envied by other nations.

It should come from this that as long as we view the self-empowered, independent and totally free individual as our ideal citizen, we will also value nations in the same way. It follows then that nations must strive to be economically independent, wealthy and applying a hight integrity to require others to respect its core traits.

So, may the view adopted by the Super Curious human be a way out of this conflict between having a truly rich life and being able to provide this to all of humanity?
