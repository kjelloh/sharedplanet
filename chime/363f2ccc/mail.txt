2) Consider some objections or inquiries to the content of the Royal Institute lecture “The Neuroscience of Consciousness – with Anil Seth”?

	==> Consider that Anil Seth does NOT take into account that the evolution process may indeed be a way of internalising truth about reality encoded in that which reality is made up of?

So, the idea is this; We have viewed the Darwinian process of evolution as a “computation" to adapt to the environment. But we may as well see it as a process where living organisms internalise “truth” about reality in such a way to enhance its own ability to effectively recurse act-in-faith to that truth in the reality in which it operates. So, the evolutionary process is more than adaptation to reality; it is internalisation of reality. But it is a special form of internalisation. It is internalisation of reality in a reduced and compressed form. The discovered truths about reality is internalised as a model encoded into a language so that it can be remembered and tested again in the future. We may say that the truth about reality is compressed into an internal form, the model, represented in language as opposed to whatever representation it had in the external reality? Where the “language” is DNA, cellular organisation, locked in chemical processes all the way up to human language (with logical and mathematical languages on the top of the hierarchy)?

With this observation we find ourselves with the realisation that the consciousness we experience to “live within” to some degree must be an internalisation of something that exists out there in the reality we interact with. 

If the reason we as living organisms continues (don’t die) to recurse (act in faith to) our internalised model of reality, is because the truth we have internalised in fact is recurse-able in the very reality in which our actions take place, then we should be able to conclude firstly that consciousness somehow is part of this recursive process and secondly that consciousness I can exist in the reality in which we operate?


	==> Consider that Anil Seth does NOT take into account that consciousness may be something we need to be able to act-in-faith in a reality of random events and emergent individual and socially constructed “Wills”?

We know that in reality events occur in ways that require us to relate to them as being generated by some random distribution. We also know that from human interaction emerges social behaviours and collective “intelligence” (just as happens in ant societies or bee colonies to name a a few examples). If we focus on an apparent super-power of humans to interact efficiently in large numbers with total strangers (see Yuval Harari writings) we should be able to draw the conclusion that we are organisms capable of “emergent oriented programming”. That is, we have internalised a model of reality that incorporates the randomness and the possible emergences in social interactions in such a way that we can take part into making new recurse-able emergent social systems come into existence?

So to try and understand the nature or and the reason for existence of consciousness we should focus on how it enables us to participate in “programming” the social reality in which we live our lives to mutual benefits? Or to use less value-tainted words - how consciousness may be highly causally interconnected with emergent social behaviour? If we use the notion of recurse-ability of our internalised “truth” about reality in the emergent social reality in which our actions take place, then we may be able to identify traits of consciousness we may otherwise be unable to see? So, for example, the reason the existence of consciousness does not seem to be required to compute how to act in a simple cause-and-effect analysis of reality - It may in fact be crucial to effectively take part in making specific social systems to emerge. Note that the key insight here is that we may have internalised “intelligence” to make social “intelligence” emerge?

Lets say what we in language relate to as “Free Will” is our internalised “program” that based on internalised “truth” tries to determine how we should act to have some social intelligent behiour emerge. And that because our social interactions are so complex that the intelligent behaviour we socially end up doing is NOT a cause-and-effect relation to our individual actions, we have evolved to be conscious of our relation to our internal “will” and the socially expressed external “will” to actually take part in what external “will” that emerges?

It seems we may then propose that what we call “Moral” behaviour is a try to, in language, represent “truth” about the correct “faiths” for our the act-in-faith recursion so that collective “ethical” social behaviour emerge?

We may even go so far to say that what we refer to as “God” is a model of such an external “Will” that we can interact with to “know” how to adjust or individual “Moral” to have have collective “Ethics” to emerge? “God” is a model of the part or our reality in which we act and that responds to our actions that we have yet to internalise but can still interrogate and negotiate with to optimise our ability to continue to recurse our own beings “hardware” and “software”?
