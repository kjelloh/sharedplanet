We don’t think there is some final meal that forever will still our hunger. But we do think there is some final truth that will still our curiosity. This is of course not true.

2) Our longing for love makes us search for ”the right one”. But of course there is no ”right one”. Love needs to be satisfied over and over by the same or by new ”lovers”.
