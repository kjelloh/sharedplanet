I like to think and speculate about how things work and how they may work in a better way. And the joy to define what ”work” means and then also what defines a ”better” way.

It is a fun and not so seldom an exciting exercise. But in the mind it is easy to try different scenarios and believe different things to be plausible ways of doing stuff. But who am I to state that I have anything to say? Who am I to say the model of understanding I build in my mind has anything to do with reality? How can I even state with even the tiniest confidence that the solutions I come up with would be even worth considering?

I swing quite easily between the excitement that - oh how great society and the human conditions could be if we only did this or that - to the opposite - oh what a fool I am to think this has anything to do with reality. What is the purpose to imagine new orders of human societies? And maybe the worst of all - what if I could have it my way and it turns out we create a new Nazi Germany, a new Stalin Roussia or a Maoe tze Tung China? They too dreamt of a ”better” society. They too was founded by - at least I like to think so - guys like me who had dreams of how the human existence could be enhanced. Anyone who travels the path of enhancing society also risks creating a monster that ends up eating up part of the population because the idea evolved into motivating horrible actions to attain the goals.
