Could it be that our ability to relate to an organisation of humans as having a will-of-its-own (even manifested in the law of a juridical person) is in fact an illusion of our brain cerated in the same way, and for the same reason, as the illusion of self and illusion of a ”deity” (any super human power of world control)?

1) We create an illusion that all our Emdividuals are in fact all aspects of a core ”I” with a set of wills and goals.  
2) In the same way we create the Illusion that a ”them”, any ”Social Creature” (any group organized round an idea, story or motivation) are in fact a core ”them” with a organized will and a set of goals.  
3) In the same way we are able to create the Illusion of a deity-like ”powers” (e.g. Gods but also ”the market”, destiny) are in fact some ”power” with a set of wills and goals.  
4) In all these cases we relate to these entities as if they where ”one” acting in faith to some truth (goals and drives).

==&gt; We imagine we through introspection may come to understand what our true ”will” and fails to see that it changes with the range of Emdividuals we can become.  
==&gt; We treat ”them” as though they act as a group following some agenda and hold them responsible when the wrong (e.g. a CEO is brought to answer for the wrong-doings of a corporation)  
==&gt; We provide for the needs of ”the market” in hope that it will bring us wealth in the form of well paid jobs and economic growth. Or we act to have destiny work in our favor or pray to a God to provide for our needs.  
…
