2) Consider how to understand and apply the "Reduce, Balance, Generate" mantra to how to live your life as well as how to interact with energy you consume?

So, the basic idea here is to focus on three aspects of "resource consumption" in the right order. So the first thing to focus on is to Reduce the amount of resources you consume. In this way you observe that it is the amount of resources you need that define what it is you need to optimise further down the processing line. If you do what you can to reduce the amount of resources you consume you have enabled more life to exist within any constraints there are on the available resources in the first place. So for living your life you can ask yourself how much money you need and what you can do to live a good life on less money. You can think about how much time you need to spend on activity you would rather not have to do so you instead can spend it on "meaningful" things. You can ask how much stuff you need to own and take care of so you can instead own the stuff you actually care for and that brings you the least of burden to look after and the most of pleasure and value  in using.

The second thing is to put in ways to Balance between availability and demand. This means how you may "store" resources when they are available and then be able to consume them when you demand it. It could be simple things like picking apples when they are rape but not eat them until winters time. But it can also mean you share resources like tools by "storing" them as commons for other to use when you don't and vice versa. We can note that this "Balancing" is what we do with money where we earn money by labor that we then store in say a bank account for later spending on stuff we can't make ourselves. A vital part of balancing is to reduce the peak demands of resource consumption. With good ability to balance between peak demands and low demands we are better off over time to have manoeuvrability in what to consume and when to consume it.

Now only thirdly should we focus on how to actually Generate resources. By putting this as the last thing to focus on we know we have already made what we could to optimise the amount of resources we need to generate. So if we have first questioned (Reduced) how much money we need for the way of life we prioritise and then also found ways to Balance the money we need to reduce the peak demands we are now optimally prepared to look at how to generate the amount of resources we need.  We may then discover that we actually do not need so much money after all? Or we may discover the time we need to spend on looking after the things we own is not so burdensome as it was before we reduced and balanced our resource consumption?


3) Consider to find ways to "better" the economic system we have to build in incitements and freedom to apply the "Reduce, Balance, Generate" model of interacting with each other and reality?

Let's start off by valuing our current economic system based on how it incites and allows us all to apply the "Reduce, Balance, Generate" model of resource consumption? As a producer in a capitalistic system you have strong incitements to reduce the cost of producing the stuff you sell. On the other hand you are also strongly incentivised to make your customers consume as much as you can make them do. So we would like the second half of this equation to be removed. We would like producers to be incentivised to both consume as little resources as possible while also trying to have us consumer needing to buy as little as possible. Well, it is easy to see that this is not possible in the current economic system as the only way to spread wealth is to make others need the services you provide being it work, investments or what have you. As am actor in a capitalist economy you need others to pay you so that you can pay others and thus enabling everyone to get by in life.

Next, what are the incitements and freedom of actors in our capitalistic economic system to Balance between resource production and resource consumption? We can observe that there seems to be strong indices that the preferred way to optimise the production chain is to have as small storage as possible and optimise the just-in-time production and delivery instead. We saw this when the Covid pandemic hit and there where no buffers of medical equipment ready to be applied but instead the system needed to switch to producing for the new demand. Now, the cost of this was of course a reduction in life quality of the ones that required medical care. Now we may argue this is a bad example as it is not clear we can know in advance what to produce and put into storage for potential future needs? There is a cost also in producing and storing resources that may never be a demand for.  On the other hand, we can argue that if we had identified in the "Reduce" step of what we require and should prioritise for a good life we did know a pandemic was imminent and something we should prepare for to be able to live with less worries and thus a higher value of life. There are a lot of things we could have done given we had taken the task of optimising a life worth living seriously. We could have restricted how much we are allowed to travel. But that is not allowed by our economic system that serves the right to compete for consumers apatite to consume travels. We could have applied quarantine protocols for travellers like we do for shipping animals over the globe. But again, this conflicts with the demand that markets must be free to provide for the demands of customers to the lowest cost of the producer. And having to waste time in quarantine on ones vacation is just not acceptable in the name of "freedom".

We can also observe that in our current economic system the first priority is to Generate resources and have customers consume as much of it as possible. And it is hard to see any incitements to frame this into first having to Reduce and Balance  resource consumption. What I can observe this is applied as an add-on by regulating authorities and public demand for "environmental friendly" production. But it seems to me this is backwards and we can at least stipulate that if it where possible we would like the very incitements of Reducing and Balancing resource consumption should be built into the very foundation of the economic system itself?
