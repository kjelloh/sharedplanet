I want to build my house to 'passive house' standard and need a cost effective way to get well insulated walls.

0) Consider the takeaway I have gotten from this information?

It seems to me that I have to be rally careful to not get moisture, condensation or water into the walls as due to their air tightness the water will have no way of escaping. This in turn means that any moisture in the wall will stay there and potentially lead to mould growth or  rotting. 

The wall will need a very tight 'warm side' and 'cold side' air and vapour tight membrane. It seems we would like to still have a semi permeable membrane as an airtight plastic film will trap moisture however well we try to seal it up. So allowing a slow transport of vapour is required for the walls to work over time.

But I feel I need to look into this 'air tightness'  aspect a bit more to decide for my house. I feel there is a risk I get fooled by product promotions and may miss the cheap, long living and simple solution should it exist?
