I watched the Veritasium video about how physics explain reality as if the speed of light is the same in all directions and thought this to be a good example of "mind the model".

1) Consider the youtube video "Why no one has measured the speed of light" (<https://youtu.be/pTn6Ewhb27k> )?

> *So, Einstein defined (stipulated or assumed) the speed of light to be the same in all directions as a basis for his relativity theories and to make it possible to model how clocks may or may not be synchronised. The Veritasium video make a good case explaining how we may in fact never know if this is true as all we have as observers is our local perception of what is going on. And thus we can only measure (experience) the round trip time it takes to exchange information with other frames of reference.*

> *Thus this blind spot of our knowing how reality really "is" may then serve as a good example of how out model of reality is at any time the only thing we have. And with that I mean with that is that when we understand reality or talk about reality we are in fact talking About reality in the form of a representation of it. And this representation is an instance of a model of reality - it is not reality as it is.*

> *So when Einstein defined the speed of light to be independent of the direction in space he was in fact just clarifying that his model of reality was one where the speed of light could be observed as if it behaved this way. Now, us being unable to tell if this is "true" is the same thing as saying that we have no way, and thus no reason, to make our model of reality one where the speed of light actually does depend on the direction. When we interact with reality we can't use such information for anything. And further, we can not observe any behaviour that would require us to know how the speed of light varied with direction to explain (be about) what we observe. Nor do we know of any inferred or deduced behaviour we can observe that would require us to know how the speed of light varies with the direction.*

> *Thus, "Mind the Model". We are able to "Be About" reality only by internalise a model of reality and then observe only the stuff the model is able to have us observe, infer or deduce.*

2) Consider how our model of reality in the form of Energy and Time actually only tell us correlations between observable behaviour, NOT that there is actually "Energy" or "Time" out there?

> *The basic idea here is that mathematical functions operating on quantities of Energy and Time can be seen as place holders to talk about correlations between behaviour. So when we say there is such a thing as "Kinetic Energy" and "Potential Energy" and that the sum of these two are constant in a loss-less system, we are in fact saying that matter behaves as if there are such a quantity we call Energy. But we can just as well just say that if we observe the speed of an object and measure the forces that pulls on the body trough time, we will observe the sum of the mathematical function for "Kinetic Energy" (Mass times the speed squared divided by 2) and the potential energy (e.g., The mass times the height times the gravitational constant g) is constant. Whether each of these quantities as separate entities actually "exist" somewhere on the body we observe is a whole other matter.*
