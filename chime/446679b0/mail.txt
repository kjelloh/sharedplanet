1) Consider in times social order transition "playing for your team" will get it wrong because you inevitably act-in-faith to the wrong model of reality?

From the observation that humans may be emergence-programmers as conscious ants we can propose that human social order is now transitioning into a new emergent behaviour. And we are on for the ride trying to make sense using models of reality that fitted historic social order. From that follows that although we are actors participating in having a new social order emerge, we are in fact totally clueless on what the new order will emerge as. It is in the very nature of an emergent behaviour in a self organising system. The rules that determines each participants behaviour has no direct causal relation to the collective behaviour.

So one may say that now is the time to NOT play for your team. If you are on the left you should NOT try to identify tactics to "win them over" because the society you are trying to win them over to is not the one that is emerging into existence. The same goes if you are on the right. The society you as a conservative are trying to protect and re-emerge is also not the one that are in fact emerging.

These are strange times. How should you act if your model of the human social reality does not fit the actual reality we are living in? Is theer even a rational answer to this question if we acknowledge that we can only reason with a cause-and-effect model while we are trying to model a reality that is about to emerge?

What observations should we feed into out rational thinking process to come up with conclusions about how to act to have a good social behaviour emerge?

5) Consider we need philosophers that can apply shamanic psycho technologies to reveal new observations of reality we need to be able to reason us to better actions?

One way to understand shamanic practices through the human history is that they proved to be valuable psycho technologies to have our brain reveal better truth about reality than the one that was self evident to us. From this we may conclude that if our contemporary society is in fact transitioning into a new emergent order, then what is self evident to us is problem the wrong way to represent was is happening, and we are thus in need of new revealed truth about reality, as this will enable to apply reasoning with better facts to most effectively participate in having a good society emerge.

How can we perform philosophical shamanic practices to reveal new truth about contemporary human reality? Well, we have theories that the shamanic psycho technologies involved confusing ourselves to disrupt the self evident. This makes sense in that it is the self evident we are questioning. To see what is hidden by the self evident view of reality we need to deprive our senses from ordinary stimulation and our brain of energy to uphold status quo. So shamans through time has practices sleep depravation, starving, exhausting exercise in the form of dancing and chanting and even taking drugs to have the mind enter some alternative state.

Now I will not dare to take any drugs of the kinds recommended for shamanic experiences. But I could imagine to try almost any other way of trying to disrupt my understanding of reality just enough to make new sense of it.

I would argue that the post structural mind-set as put forward by post modern thinkers can be viewed as a form of philosophical shamanism. Only that they did not self identify as shamans. But to its core the whole post modern critique of truth can be viewed as a shamanic approach to see past the self evident. What can we reveal from post modern shamanism? Well, I think post modern thinkers points very strongly at the pain that humans experience in the society that emerges from modernity. And although Marx, Lacan or Hegel (the heroes of contemporary far left movements) can be strongly questioned about how their analysis and call for action can be trusted to actually have a society with less pain emerge, the pain caused by modernity is true and must be adressed.
