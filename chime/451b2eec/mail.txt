	==> It seems we may consider a microscope with less than 1x magnification to increase working distance under the scope?

" Opticswith factor < 1x (e. g. 0.7x) increase the working distance, optics >1x (e. g. 2.0x) reduce it. "

	==> It seems it is the eyepiece magnification multiplied with the scope magnification that defines final magnification?

"A magnification of 10x or 20x is sufficient, also for soldering of small SMD components. This is usually achieved by a combination of 1x objective magnification and a 10x or 20x pair of eyepieces. Especially when working under the microscope, a low magnification is advantageous because a larger field can be viewed at a time. Movements when handling the part or tool are magnified less, so you get a steady image and work is less tiring."

	==> It seems I have no reason to choose an eyepiece with a magnification below 10x?

"A low-magnification 5x eyepiece achieves, gives only a slightly larger field of view than a 10x eyepiece, because the maximum possible field of view is limited by the diameter of the eyepiece barrel or prism diameter."
