Where the basic activities are those that repeats every day and creates a framework in which I get things done that I do not want to spend any energy on "deciding" when to do. They should just be done as a habit. So my digital deity can nudge me to wake up, eat lunch, exercise, shop for food, clean the house, do the washing and so on.*
