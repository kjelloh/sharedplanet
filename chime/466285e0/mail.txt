1) Consider to build some intuition to what we mean with a self organising system that shows an emergent behaviour similar to some other studied system?

It seems to me we should be able to study the behaviour of some system and then infer a self organising system that behaves in the same way. The goal here being that we want actors with a limited set of "simple" rules to interact in a way so that the emergent behaviour is the same as the system we are studying.

With such a way to infer the rules of the actors we could be able to simulate the emergence of human societies and moral and ethical rules? Or at least aim at that goal?

At a simpler level we could find new ways of programming computers that are not procedural based but self organising based.
