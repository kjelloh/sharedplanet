The Super Curious view of truth is that there will always be a better, a more true, a more beneficial or deeper truth to be found. The flaws of our human abilities to grasp or find the final and complete truths about our existence and the universe will forever hinders us to ”get there”.

1) But our Curiosity is not wired that way. It is wired to seek the final truth. Sure, it is easily blinded into thinking something to be true while it is in fact not, the drive is still the final truth. Does this mean the Super Curious has to deny Curiosity the very thing it seeks? And in that case, how does that make the Super Curious feel?  
2) Consider to propose that the Super Curious may still allow herself to seek the final truth. The Super Curious may hold the fact that we will never grasp or know the final truth is in itself a final truth?  
3) Write about the paradox of holding it true that truth can never be fully uncovered or grasped with the belief that that truth is in fact in itself a final truth! Can the Super Curious allow herself to doubt also this core truth? Is this paradox a necessity is defined by Gödels theorem (Any complete logical system will always express statements that are paradoxes)?
