	==> It seems the term “Knolling” was first introduced in 1987 by Andrew Kromelov? 

“The term 'knolling' was first used in 1987 by Andrew Kromelow, a janitor at Frank Gehry's furniture fabrication shop”

“Sachs spent two years in Gehry's shop as a fabricator and adopted use of the term from Kromelow."

	==> It seems Sachs has adopted Knolling as an important part of his creative process?

“BULLET VIII: ALWAYS BE KNOLLING (ABK)
Scan your environment for materials, tools, books, music, etc. which are not in use.
Put away everything not in use. If you aren't sure, leave it out.
Group all 'like' objects.
Align or square all objects to either the surface they rest on, or the studio itself.
"

	==> It seems My strategy to put everything I own for easy access is “Knolling" but without the “put away” part of stuff I do not use?
