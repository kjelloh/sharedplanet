	==> The chip seems to come in variants for TMLC, CanOpen or CoE interface?

	==> CAN seems to stand for Controller Area Network (https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controller_Area_Network <https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controller_Area_Network>)?
	==> CanOpen seems to be an industri standard protocol for network communation in automation systems (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CANopen <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CANopen>)?
	==> CoE seems to stand for “CAN over Ethernet” (https://www.ethercat.org/en/technology.html#1.9.1 <https://www.ethercat.org/en/technology.html#1.9.1>)?

1.9.1 CAN application protocol over EtherCAT (CoE)  <>
With the CoE protocol, EtherCAT provides the same communication mechanisms as in CANopen®-Standard EN 50325-4: Object Dictionary, PDO Mapping (Process Data Objects) and SDO (Service Data Objects) – even the network management is similar.
