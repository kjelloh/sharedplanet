Instead of scheduling tasks with set goals, consider to instead schedule focal points with defined goals of interest.

Look at how I myself use to write Todo-mails to myself. They are maybe not so much taks with a goal as they are items of interest. Each Todo seems to define something interesting to study and with a suggested way of communicating this foal point to others. Sometimes the gaol is to write something about a subject. Others suggests trying to build an application. Just very seldom is the Todo about an actual task with a specified goal like ”buy this book”.

So, maybe a virtual secretary (coach, diety) should actually schedule focal points, when to focus my attention and what to focus on. The actual goal of such attention may not be the key issue here. Rather a rare exception. The general reason to do anything is out of interest to attend to ones desires!
