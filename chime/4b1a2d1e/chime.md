The Super Curious realizes that we will never ”get there” and can thus find what relaxation that is possible at ”where we are”. The reason to become Super Curious lays in realizing the impossible situation of the Curious search for the final destination.

1) The Curious tries to get the truly happy life. There must be a way to live that is the best one possible. Thus the curious is never satisfied with the current state of life as there are always flaws indicating she is not ”there yet”.  
2) The Curious tries to get enlightenment, the insight into the absolute truth about things. Thus the Curious can never be satisfied about the truths we currently believe as it is just so easy to spot their flaws or groundlessness. The Curious is then forced to live in frustration created by the insatiable curiosity. An unsuitability the Curious may handle by either trying not to think to much about these questions or by trying to find a state of mind where curiosity can’t be felt. The latter may be accomplished by applying mindfulness or to seek enlightenment, in both cases one is assumed to get rid of curiosity.  
3) The Curious has to struggle with change as the goal is to ”get there” and if we has succeeded to ”get here” we do not change to make us leave again. The Curious can only accept change one initiates to ”get there”, never change that makes us drift "somewhere else". The only change acceptable to the Curious is ”Progress”, all oner types of change is bad. Thus the Curious is either destined to favor conservatism or some controlled progress as scientific progress, technological progress or progress of society as laid out by liberal or social political ideologies. As neither of these actually has a strategy to cope with unwanted or unplanned change the Curious is forced to continuously struggle to control change with the aim to either create status quo or to direct change so we archive our goals. As the proposed goals and means are inevitable different between different Curious the result often becomes futile struggles between Curious ideologies instead of actual actions to better the society (e.g. the left wing and right wing struggles we see in most western countries).
