We do not have a core or a true individual self with a set of fixed or semi-fixed properties. Instead all observations of our nature suggests that the inner and by others experienced personality actually springs to existence stimulated buy the event at hand. That is, our brain reacts to sensoratory input by creation a suitable consciousness to handle the current state of affairs.

Now, how can we understand this? It contradicts our experience that we, ourselves, and our friends seems to expose a set of identifiable treats that we recognize and know to be owned by that exact individual? Well, one way to understand how it is possible is that given that when we interact with a known friend, the state of affairs that occurs is very much similar from time to time, this both ourselves and our friends creates a consciousness that is also very similar each time. Also for our own experience, as long as we do not find ourselves in a radical new situation we will repeatedly create a consciousness with very similar properties over and over.

On the other hand, when we experience a very new and overwhelming situation that calls for action (like a threat or very strong feeling of affection or anger) we would not be surprised if ”new sides of me” shows up. We like to think these are actually hidden properties of our core that seldom springs to life, but we may as well see them as a totally new consciousness created for this very new satte of affairs.

Further, there is nothing in current knowledge or observation that suggests there must be something (a core true self) from which our consciousness springs to life. That is just an illusion created by our brain to hide the blind spots we actually have when studying the world and our inners clockwork. One main function of the brain is to fill in the gaps so that what we expect is what we experience, thus enabling us to detect only what is important for us to move around in the world.

A more accurate way of describing is is that we are actually dividuals, each and every time showing a different one as needed by the current state of affairs.
