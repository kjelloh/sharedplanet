Could it be possible to invent a currency for ”happiness” and then evaluate goods and services in this ”happiness” currency?

1) A new iPhone could then be valued as 5 happiness money whilst the service of watering your neighbor plats could be values 10 happiness money?  
2) Happiness money could then be defined to deteriorate over time with different speed. The happiness money from buying an iPhone could deteriorate faster than the happiness money earned from helping your neighbor.  
3) Happiness value of goods and services would be very dependent on who does the evaluation. An iPhone would make person A happier than person B for various reasons.  
4) Happiness money would behave very differently from ordinary money. For example, your would not be able to trade happiness money with someone else. If you have 10 happiness money you can’t give them to someone else just like that. But it could still work as a way to evaluate possible happiness pay-offs and be used as a guidance to invest in happiness? One could for example argue that it would be better to invest in lower happiness values that lasts over longer time? Or invest in goods or services that may even give you a long high return of happiness.  
5) Happiness money could be a way to study and learn the happiness value of actions. Even if the happiness value may be hard to prize, it could still at least be an indication of what is generally to be considered as higher happiness and what happiness that is considered to last longer.

What can this kinds of mind games lead to? Is there some value waiting to be discovered in this type of reasoning?
