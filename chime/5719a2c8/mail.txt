It just came to me that it could be fun to model traditional left vs right argumentation as corresponding to lego building vs clay building?

1) Consider traditional left thinking focus on care and the importance of freedom from oppression and border?

	==> Consider that trying to reason about how it should feel to live means you must imagine people to be able to change (clay thinking)?

	…

2) Consider the thinking of the right focus on security, structure and order and second thought before changing that which works?

	==> Consider trying to reason about how “what is” or “what has been” means you must imagine people to be resistant to change (brick thinking)?

3) Consider a nice touch to this analogy is that bricks are actually made from clay (once mouldable now fix in shape)?

	…

4) Consider clay vs brick reasoning also seems isomorphic to the extremes we see where extreme left abolish biology (brick facts) and extreme right abolish immigration (clay facts)?

	…

5) Consider to argue we need both brick and clay reasoning about humans to get along in recurs-able acts-in-faith?

	==> If all human structures and interactions are “brick like” we stagnate into rigidity (fails to learn and adapt in bad times to reach good times)?

	==> If all humans structures and interactions are “clay like” we collapse into mud (fails to remember bad times through good times)?
