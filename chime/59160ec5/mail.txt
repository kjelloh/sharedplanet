Consider to write a ”manual” about requirement communication and administration based on my almost 20 years of experience.

1) Requirement ID enabling iterative enhancement (the sweet spot between control and flexibility)
2) Flavors of requirements (Must, Should, May)
3) The importance of stating who is required to do what and when.
4) The importance of taking a small step when a large one is not yet possible
5) The power of stating an imperfect requirement to find the best one (trusting that imperfect requirements will be questioned so a better one may later be stated)
6) Different ways of stating requirements (stand-alone, cross referenced and sub-requirement lists)
7) Top down from product level to technical implementation.
8) The importance to select the right model and understanding the difference between the model and reality (applying the same requirements to different realizations)
8.1) The trick of defining a model that is transparently applied to reality (How a special kind of computer is identified as a special kind of HOST)
9) The importance of aiming at a changing and unknown future (continuous re-aiming applying iterative corrections)
10) The importance to automate to free humans to spend more time being creative (you can’t automate creativity).
11) The power of separating what you say from how you say it (The same requirements may be manifested in document, tests and community knowledge).
12) Preferring Right to Not-Wrong (Much easier to comply to What-must-be than What-must-not-be)
13) The importance to allow stated truths to be more elaborated that spoken day-to-day language (Stated "Approval Reversal” may be spoken about as just ”Reversal”)
14) The trick to find a model that many audiences understand (The Product, Processors, Card Schemes and Terminal Vendors may use different terms but the same model)
15) The importance to understand how to meet business, technical and security requirements in a changing world (having satisfied customers whilst maximizing a sustainable profit)
16) How to improve whilst everything is on fire (Being able to solve problems, i.e. put out fires,  so they do not come back or live on in a worse form)
17) Understanding why the right way is not the best way (Decide how to move forward in a world of hidden agendas and unknown legacies)
18) Understand how to open and close ”doors” to create change in preferable direction (the magic of making the right things happen by making them possible)
