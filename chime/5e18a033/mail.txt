Listening to Swedish ”Filosofiska Rummet” (http://t.sr.se/1GLjZE5) one reflection is stated which is interesting - the optimist and the pessimist has the same goal, namely to cope with reality in the best way.

Theres a couple of things in the program worth following up on.

1) Humans are wired to be over confident in their own abilities (and thus under confident in others?). This would indicate that overconfidence is something favored by evolution. Now, this is not saying being an optimist in general is a good thing, but being optimistic in ones own abilities seems to be. Thus, although the optimist and the pessimist has the same goal, optimism seems the best way to go when it comes to evaluating ones own actions?
2) If evolution seems to favor an optimistic (untrue) view of ones own abilities, then what does this say about the value of the truth? In the light that evolutions also drives true perception of reality extinct we have two examples where the truth is not most beneficial to us?
3) In modern world we like to think self empowerment through optimistic thinking is the way to go. But this would be to favor true evaluation of reality but go for a more wishful thinking approach to get things done to our favor? What does this say about the value if finding the truth?

Could one say that the truth is still good to know but from there an optimistic or pessimistic view of the outcome of our actions is open for grabs?
