
For there days now I have been ready to start cutting the wood for my indoor shed.
But I have not been able to decide on a circular saw miter jig to build to make the cuts.
Something has not felt right. And my brain seem to have been unable to wrap itself round the problem and give me a solution.
There are a lot of circular saw jigs on youtube. But to different degrees they just felt to complicated.
Or was I just to lazy to get going?
Anyhow, After scouting youtube for circular saw jigs I finally decided to go with a very simple jig that you just place on the piece you want to cut.
And with that decision I went to my work bench and started to play with the material I had.
-
The first thing I noticed was that my circular saw had the blade on the left side of the sole.
This means that the jig had to be made so the cuts are made to the left of the cut piece.
Otherwise the saw will rest on the piece I cut off.
This in turn means I will have to operate the saw with my left hand.
No bigger problemens there. But a bit surprising. 
And yet another example of how my brain fails to simulate reality. 
I need to do physical thinking - that is, play with the real physical world to get to the correct solution!
