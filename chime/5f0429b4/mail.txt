When we discuss what bad things kapitalism and modern society brings (over consumption, isolation, discrimination, inequality and the unsustainability of it all) it is tempting but probably not viable to propose that the solution is bringing back the ”old ways”?

1) Yuval Hariri talks about how society changed from being family/community centric (transactions of favors) to kapitalistic centric (all transactions valued in money) (”Familjens och lokalsammhällets sammanbrott” sid 344).
2) This makes it tempting to ”cure” the evils of kapitalism with longing for family and community bonds again?
3) But in the face of Emdividualism we can’t choose to act-in-faith to these old values by free will? We need to change our core believes so that our act-in-faiths makes a society emerge where we as Emdividuals may experience those values again?
4) Also, as Super Curious we know that we don’t know and must try to see what happens rather than plan for a fixed way of things? AS Emdividuals we understand we must state some truths about the wrongs about current society and then try things out ”Worker Space” way?
…

Consider to elaborate on arguments why we can’t solve problems of current society with static truths about ways in the past that was ”better" (both the society and the emdividuals that comes into being are emergent phenomena and is hard to predict from the sum of all act-in-faith we do)?
