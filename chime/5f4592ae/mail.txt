Listening to Paul VanderKlay commenting Bret Weinstein on religion I picked up Bret asking if we should be governed by what we know and though that was interesting, because... well why?

So at around 56:58 into youtube video "What is Religion? Bret Weinstein, Tom Holland, Sam Harris, Jim Rutt" (https://youtu.be/0aV5WtmIt3U?t=3419) Bret responds "...if we are going to get people to listen to us about the necessity of moving towards a system in which we are governed by what it is we know ...".

This made me ask myself - Should we be governed by "what we know" or the "unknown". In some sense under the critique of religion lays the very notion that No, we should NOT be governed by the unknown. The enlightenment can be summarised as the "sacred ignorance" with the twist that we must NOT base any actions on things we do not know. Ignorance is sacred in the sense that it must NOT guide action. The unknown does not care for us!  Religion on the other hand is based on a model of reality where the unknown actually does care for us. There is God. God cares. We can interact with God. We can interrogate the unknown through religious practices.

Which approach to reality is "the best". Well, at least we should be honest and admit that the scientific method restricts us to an island we "know" without any real tools to take leap of faith into the unknown. Trusting only the scientific reality we risk living on a "too small Island" to live meaningful lives! Religion on the other hand has shown to be too unyielding (too slow) to make room for "the known". Trusting only Religious reality we risk living on some insuffrior part of the Island.

Still I think we should model reality to enable us to be governed by the "Unknown" as this by definition makes our reality larger than the one the scientific reality allows us to trust. But we aslo need to update our "search engine" to interrogate the unknown and share the knowledge and our experiences to have our collective interaction with the unknown self organise into some optimal emerged social construction.
