1) Consider to design a facade using green house glass panels?

==&gt; It seems for outdoor glazing the preferred materials are Acrylic or Polycarbonate (<https://www.curbellplastics.com/Research-Solutions/Industry-Solutions/Challenging-Environments/Outdoor> )?

> UV resistant grades of [acrylic](https://www.curbellplastics.com/Research-Solutions/Materials/Acrylic) and [polycarbonate](https://www.curbellplastics.com/Research-Solutions/Materials/Polycarbonate)sheet are frequently used for outdoor glazing. Both materials are less than half the weight of glass and have better impact resistance than glass. Acrylic sheet offers the advantage of superior light transmission compared with polycarbonate. Additionally, thermoformable grades of acrylic sheet generally require less drying prior to thermoforming compared with polycarbonate. Polycarbonate has superior toughness compared with acrylic and it is often specified when [outstanding impact strength and durability](https://www.curbellplastics.com/Research-Solutions/Materials/Polycarbonate) are required.

> #### Outdoor glazing applications for plastics include:

- Skylights
- Sun room windows
- Security glazing for embassies and detention centers
- Bus shelter glazing
- Barrel vaults and canopies
- Windows for military vehicles
- Windows for construction, agricultural, and mining equipment

> #### Material options for outdoor glazing applications:

> Acrylic – [OPTIX®](https://www.curbellplastics.com/Shop-Materials/All-Materials/Acrylic/OPTIX-Acrylic-Sheet-Clear), DURAPLEX® (impact modified)

> Polycarbonate – TUFFAK® (formerly called Makrolon®), [TUFFAK® SL](https://www.curbellplastics.com/Shop-Materials/All-Materials/Polycarbonate/Makrolon-Polycarbonate-Sheet-Sign-Grad), [TUFFAK® 15](https://www.curbellplastics.com/Shop-Materials/All-Materials/Polycarbonate/Makrolon-15-Polycarbonate-Sheet)

> [SentryGlas® Interlayer](https://www.curbellplastics.com/Research-Solutions/Materials/SentryGlas-Interlayer) – used as a bonding layer between sheets of glass for security glazing, hurricane windows, open-edged railings, facades, and canopies
