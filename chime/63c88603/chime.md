Bitcoin transaction authorization is based on a mathematical race where the winner of the longest block-chain is the one to trust (e.g. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l9jOJk30eQs). A forger must "out-race" the whole network which just costs to much.

a) Can we implement the same mechanism to validate information and build truth-webs?

b) For example, can we leverage on this kind of thinking to create a network of information about product and services environmental foot-print?

c) In that case, how do we design such a network so that the ”truth” about the actual foot-print wins and is not ”forged” by strong economical interests that want their product get good but false ratings?

d) Seems we would need to evaluate how ”trustworthy" the information provider is by their history and how spread the user trust is in terms of divergent users. A company shall not be able to ”buy” or create enough users to outvote the network?

e) Can we be inspired by how scientific ”truth” are validated? Scientific truths relies on independent repetition of experiments and studies. Thus, if we build a truth web we may trust nodes of ”truths” (in this case information about production foot-print data) that have a large number of explicit and implicit relations to other trusted nodes in the web. A node with few or weak relations to other nodes should carry weak trust?
