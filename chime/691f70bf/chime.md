This was the day ants got self conscious!

It began as any other day. Nothing out of the ordinary was going on. In the morning, as the temperature raised, the scout ants went out on their normal strolling around the anthill. Triggered by the light and the temperature and the amount of signalling chemicals they received from ants the met. The nursing ants went on with their task of tending to the eggs and the hatched ants. Some fed the ant queen that just endlessly produced new eggs that the nursing ants carefully placed in the nursing chambers. Soldier ants went on their business to detect any threats. Yes, All went on with their taks just as ants had done for million of years. Acting in faith to the truth about how reality should be as created by processing the signalling chemicals they received from fellow ants and their sensory inputs from its environment. They did not know it but together they made up the ant society in all its complexity. But they had no supervisor. Even the queen was unaware she even was a queen. The soldier ants had no notion or internal representation of being soldiers. As far as humans who studied these ant colonies could tell, the whole shebang of ant society intelligent behaviour was a carefully balanced emergence of individual ants communicating about the reality of their surroundings and the internal processes required to have the ant society continue to recurse its meaning of life trough history.

Then the magic happened! For reasons still unknown to any intelligent organism in the universe one of the ants gained self consciousness! Out of the blue it suddenly experienced being aware of its own existence. Still, it gained self consciousness only as an addition to all the internal notions about reality it previously had. So although self conscious it still had no language. It knew nothing about symbols or abstract representation about reality. It just gained a spark of experience that enabled it to self reflect. In its tiny body, somehow, it now had the ability to represent itself as a separate entity from the others. It also had the ability to relate itself to the whole ant society. The very ability to internalise a self reflecting self also enabled the little ant to create a notion of another ant having the same internal clockwork. To the little ant this very change cause a surge of re-organisation in its internal processing of reality. Before it had been contempt with processing chemical signals from other ants and sensory input about light, heat, moist, food and so on. Now in addition it was able to alter this processing based on a representation of itself as a self contained entity with these capabilities. It was now aware of the urges to act as a result of its experience of the surrounding reality. What was it to do in reaction to this awareness. What was the purpose of being able to be self aware. Because, to be honest, it still had no idea about why it had the urges it had. What was its purpose? It seemed to the little ant that in this new reality of being self aware it found itself totally baffled by the options it provided. Was it even options? Before it has just unconsciously processed input form the external reality and acted out the urge for action that this processing resulted in. Now it was aware of the urge to act that resulted from transforming the experienced reality into a conclusion of what had to be changed. So the first thing the ant consciously was made aware of, was that it it constantly seemed to try and understand how reality was and then try to determine how it ought to be. Curious!

Well, the emergence of the little ants self awareness started a series of revolutionary re-organisations of how it internally processed reality. It was still an ought-producing organism. But it now had gained a new dimension for trying to determine what to do. Because its reality now had changed to also include the existence of itself as a self aware little ant, its ought producing algorithm had changed! Now remember that the little ant still had no language in which to represent such thinking. Never the less, the self awareness was there as an experience. And just by being there it affected how the ant experienced reality. It was all very Wonderfull but bewildering!
