Trying to learn the harmonies of ”When you gotta learn” I spotted some patterns that may be good to know when playing these kinds of jazzy chords.

1) There is plenty of Cm9, Dm9, Fm9 and what have you. On piano its easier to read these as Ebmaj7/C, Fmaj7/D, Abmaj7/F and so on.  
2) That is, each m9 chord is actually its minor third up major 7 chord over its base (e.g. Cm9 == Ebmaj7/C)  
3) Maybe the m9-chords are used as the guitar chords? That is, on guitar you play a Cm9 when the harmony is Ebmaj7/C (The base plays the C, the piano the Ebmaj7)?
