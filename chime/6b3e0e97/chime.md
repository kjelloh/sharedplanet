This site that wants to build a community to stop students from smoking (http://www.thetruth.com) seems like a site for inspiration on how to communicate using a ”cool” attitude and credible intuitions?

1) The site promotes to ”X your profile” meaning one should tag ones profile picture with a logotypes ’X’. This is the same idea as we think SPC should promote. Consider to user this site approach as an inspiration?  
2) In the middle of the site they use some cool graphics and animations to show the decline trend in number of smoking youths. This talks to emotions and ignites a drive to ”join a cool trend”? How can we find and communicate the same ”good trends” for SPC actions?  
3) The site feels ”crisp”, cool and to the point. Why is that? Can we use this as inspiration for SPC and SPB sites? The design seems to be based on large text, images and designed artifacts. There seems to be no details that interfere with the main message? Is that the thing?  
4) The state ”We are here to empower, not judge” (http://www.thetruth.com/about). Thats exactly the message of SPC. Can we reuse this ”truth” to leverage emergence of beneficial change in general (i.e. stop smoking as well as stop overconsumption and production)?
