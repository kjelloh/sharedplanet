One way of understanding what this systems is about is to think about how it might have been when we lived in small societies as gatherers and hunters. At each time the group decided what was needed to get and hunt for. This made up the bulk need of the whole community. One can suspect that the hunters and gatherers then when out to bring what was needed to the community. The hunted and gathered was then distributed to members of the community for consumption of production of food and tools. One can also image that each individual may also have traded their part for other services within the community. At the very least, the consumption was limited to the need of the community and the ability to obtain resources (time and labour).

Now, in mother economic system we share the production using a neutral value system called money. We provide labour in exchange for money that we then may use the obtain goods and services. As opposed to pre-money societies where the goods and services was shared directly based on availability and probably the effort put in. Of course, we can only speculate. But at the very least we do still naturally and without thinking participate in these types of small community non-money inflated activities. Take for example a party gathering where the guests provides food, entertainment and labour sharing. No money is needed to decide who is going to do what. Based on pure reasoning and the common agreement to make a great party each and every one contributes where is needed or where leader or leaders instructs work to be done. As long as each sense that each contributes with roughly the same amount of resources and work everyone is satisfied.
