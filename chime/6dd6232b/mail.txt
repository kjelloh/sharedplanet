	==> It seems M.2 and PCIe are form factors (not to be confused with electrical interface)?

“M.2 is just the form factor. M.2 drives can come in SATA versions (like the Crucial MX300 M.2 for example) and NVMe versions (like the Samsung 960 Pro), which describes the bus they use to electrically communicate with the other PC components."
