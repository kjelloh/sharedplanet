Consider to propose a way to gather people to share a workspace with by ”swarming” based on interests and collaboration needs. This could be an extension of the idea of open open office rents and working in public spaces.

1) Swarming occurs as the result of posting needs for work mates and where one would like that to happen. The social network then ”swarm” to locations based on iterative choices of where to go.
2) Swarming may happen from day to day or remain fixed for longer periods until new needs make swarm organisation to a tipping point and  a new emergence occur.
