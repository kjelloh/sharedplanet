I keep coming back to Sam Harris vs Jordan B Petersson "Truth" definition and how to understand them.

So the idea here is to view the "Truth" that Jordan B Petersson talks about (and that triggers Sam Harris so much) is closer to what we call "Wisdom" than what we normatively call True? We can propose this if we note that what Jordan B Petersson talks about is "Truth" as "That which serves life". This definition brings him and Sam Harris to the heated debate on truth where Harris took the example of smallpox research. Sam claimed we can get to the truth about smallpox regardless of wether the research would kill by leaking out of the laboratory. And Petersson clang on to the notion that No, what we know about smallpox can be false if it turns out it kills us.

Now, here we can see that Petersson is closer to the notion of Wisdom. Is it wise to perform research on smallpox? Well, if it ends up killing people it may not be so wise.

Also, in the smallpox example we can see that Harris talks about what is true about smallpox at any specific point in time. While Peterson talks more about what survives over time. If, for some reason, those who take part in smallpox research as a result of their knowledge and activity, dies, then the "truth" about smallpox does not survive over time and is thus "false" according to Petersson. It is true at some moment in time but it is false over time (it dies as it fails to survive).
