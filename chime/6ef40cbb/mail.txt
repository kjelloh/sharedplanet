Could we argue that the modern Multiverse Theory is in fact just a variant on the theme “God rules in mysterious ways”?

1) Both state that to ask “why” about our existence is a false question in that it has no answer. 

	==> In the God-case we will forever be out-of-reach to the goals of God and should not ask.
	==> In the multiverse case we will forever by out-of-reach because there is no answer (we just happen to live in a universe out of the infinite ones where we are able to ask the question).

2) Both proposes a truth that is not falsifiable about our existence that makes the “why” question a false one. We cant prove that God does not exist. And we can’t prove that we do not live in one of an infinite number of possible universes.

	==> The God theory is not falsifiable because Gods existence requires absence of proof. If there where proof of Gods existence then faith would not be required. Thus there is no way to look and determine that God does not exist.
	==> The multiverse theory is not falsifiable because each universe is totally isolated from all others. So there is no way for us to “look” and determine that we are alone.

3) Both provide a “straw man” argument in that it answers another question than the one asked. So instead of answer the “why” question, it provides another answer that answers why the “why” question is of not interest to us.

	==> The God “mysterious ways” argument is a "straw man" in that it answers “why this existence” with another one, namely “why we can never know”.
	==> The multiverse “it just happens to be so” argument is a "straw man” in that it answers “why this existence” with another one, namely “why we can never know”.

4) Both answers fails to close the “why” question about our existence as the provided answer can’t be settled and thus may be doubted. Both provide an answer that fails to satisfy our curiosity.

	==> The God Theory is valid only if God exists AND Gods intentions are mysterious. This is actually two “truths” about which we can’t know but must trust.
	==> The multiverse theory is valid only if it is actually true AND this one just happened to be cerated in a way so that we can exist. This is actually two “truths” about which we can’t know but must trust.

5) Both answers has act-in-faith benefits and therefore provides value to our interaction with “reality”

	==> Acting-In-Faith to Gods “mysterious ways” helps us accept-and-move-on, both as persons and collaboratively.
	==> Acting-In-Faith to Multiverse “it just so happens” helps helps us accept-and-move-on, both as persons and collaboratively.

6) None of the answers can serve as any further predictions about our existence in this universe.

	==> Gods “mysterious ways” means just that we are not supposed to know, and thus we can’t.
	==> The Multiverse “it just so happens” provides us with nothing new about the universe we happens to live in.

7) Both are examples of how we construct circular arguments to circumference the inner void of our knowledge.

	==> God created the world. The world is mysterious because of Gods “mysterious ways”.
	==> This universe came to be by chance. We just happens to be in a universe where we may exist and observe it.
