For fun, play with the idea that perhaps there is a kind of ”stockholmssyndrom” that makes us defend the very idea that kidnapped us and does not act for our good.

Zygmunt Bauman (http://www.bokus.com/bok/9789171732781/konsumtionsliv/ , http://www.svd.se/vi-har-en-svar-tid-med-stora-offer-framfor-oss and http://www.ur.se/Produkter/176736-Larandets-idehistoria-Vad-ar-kunskap) seems to talk about ”men inte vill eller vågar erkänna: att vi långtifrån är så fria som vi inbillar oss... Med ockuperade sinnen sjunger vi ockupantens lov.”. This is a kind of stockholm syndrome is it not?
