The super curious news demands to know how the reported news fits into the grander schemes of things. The super curious knows that any such attempt may be more or less accurate but to not attempt to do that at all is worse. The world is a big place and a lot of stuff is going on. It is just impossible for any-one to build an accurate view of what is really going on, why this and that happened and finally how that may impact the life in my surroundings and my own life today and in the future. Still, the super curious is interested to hear what others with better knowledge in different areas can make out from what is going on. Also, the super curious is interested to get news that to actually ”matters” to the interests of my life. Just blurting out reports of events based on how horrible, how unusual or how different they are from what happened yesterday is simply to lame and leaves the super curious human to much in the dark about the state of society.
