
So, the basic idea is to identify that we experience reality both on a subconscious level and on a conscious relational level.

So it seems we would benefit from being able to name objects we model in reality as beings of unconscious or conscious source.

So lets say we use the "em-" prefix to denote "objects" that we model as being self-orgnaised and emerging. So the Emdividual is how we unconsciously model ourselves. It can be how we identify sexually, as a person, our religious preferences and so on. We can then also talk about emtheocracies as our experience (observation) of humans acting together driven by a common goals. But where we identify that these cooperative ecosystems is self-organised though care-driven actions of humans that, when asked, will explain there behaviour as "self-evident".

We may then use the counter prefix "syn-" for the same "em-prefixed-objects" but where we denote them with a rational and consciously believable story and abstract model. So the syndividual is our rational and conscious explanation of how we identify and explain who we are. And a Syntheocracy is our rational and conscious model and explanation of collective cooperation by humans.

So if you identify as heterosexual your emdividual is expressed as you experience heterosexual attraction while your identification as a heterosexual is your rational syndividual model (observation and explanation).

In the same way you experience a care for black lives matter as an expression of a emsyntheocracy (your unconscious model and representation of this "object") while you observe and explain the black lives matter syntheocracy as your rational model and explanation.

We can now propose that every observation we make have a sub-conscious, self-organising and emergent em-part and a conscious, rational created and explained  syn-part.

And we may use these terms to make a syper-syn-model of all em-objects and syn-objects we already have. Thus creating a model of the human experience and her role in self-realisation and cooperation with which to observe and model all human observations and activities?

Our individual "theos" is our model of what we care about and thus what we observe "should" become real and manifest.

Our collective "theos" is our model of what we care about together to cooperate for what collectively "should" become real and manifest.

Individualism now becomes a syntheocracy where the individual freedom is placed as superseding the collective needs.

Communism now becomes a syntheocracy where the collective needs is placed as superseeding the individual needs.

We can now be tempted to talk about a "supersyntheocracy" or "metasyntheocracy" where Individualism and communism (and more -isms) fit our em-syn-model of these sub-models?
