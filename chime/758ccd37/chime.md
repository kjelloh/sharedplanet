Would it not be fun if there was an easy way to turn your body powers into energy to be used at home?

1) Design a compact ”machine” that you at least can drive with your big muscle groups (bicycling, rowing) to change a battery.  
2) Make it modular so you may select different battery packages and generator power.  
3) Let it have USB, 12V and power network outlets (220V, 110V etc) to charge phones, lap-tips and also drive household ecquipmentsm tooth brushes, shavers and so on.  
4) Let it have internet connection to exchange information about generated energy and to interact in challenges (competions) of different forms.  
5) Consider it to provide Darwinet support?  
6) let it have interface to Teslas wall mount power pack (http://www.teslamotors.com/powerwall)  
7) Let the user interface show available time and effect stored. Also allow setting of effect and time and let the machine automatically turn on power outlet when that amount of energy is available. In this way the user may set e.g. 1 KW for 2 minutes and then keep pedaling until the machine turns on the outlet.  
8) Power outlet much be controlled so that it is not runded on until it is reasonable able to provide power for a reasonable time. Otherwise the connected apparatus may be damaged?!
