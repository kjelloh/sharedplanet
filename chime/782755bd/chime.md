I am trying hard to reverse the way we understand truth and one way is to propose when we argue for a "Truth" we are in fact synthesise (emulate or compute) predicted observations and then take that as confirmation if they fot the observations we care for.

> *So the basic idea here is that we do NOT in fact deduce the truth form our observations but it is much more the other way around. We internalise a model of the world that in some unconscious representation signals to us how to experience and act in this modelled reality. And when we "search" for the truth we take the observations already generated by our model of it and then apply reasoning to argue for how well our model fits reality. And the thing we are in fact doing when we reason is to emulate reality from arguments. So our arguments are the input to our model (truth) and the output (the deductions) are the predicted observations.*

> *The reason this is not obvious to us is that we can't twist this truth searching too far from the observations. Especially when the observations made by our fellow humans are to different from the one we are making. But from contemporary polarisation on political, ideological and religious ideas we seem to be able to observe that we hold on to our models of reality even when others model reality very different from our own.*

> *We can see this ability or ours to model reality very differently without starting to suspect there is something missing in our model from the postmodern social justice (cultural neo marxist), or what have you, model of reality as a power struggle between toxic masculinity and oppressed feminine or oppressed genders and cultures versus conservative, liberal, free market, orderly and all the way to fascist, white supremacy, nationalistic model of reality as a competence hierarchy based on tradition, biologically bounded sexes and competitive behaviour. If in fact we observe and explain reality such that we can explain and argue for the things we care for it all makes sense why these two realities seems to be noth beleivable and observable at the same time. Some common "reality" out there can be made to fit these two very different explanations of the same.*
