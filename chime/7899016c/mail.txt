Consider to make ”Super Curiosity” into an idea-driven movement.

1) The Super Curious realizes that no human, nor humanity as a whole will ever be able to find a final truth. Thus the Super Curious best relation to the truth is to validate current truth in relation to the alternative truths and the tries to pick the one that best fits all knowledge as a whole.
2) The Super Curious grades truth based on how well integrated it is into the web of truths. The Super Curious is very thorough to identify truths at the edge of the web. These truths are ungrounded and must be identified as such. The Super Curious realizes that any knowledge we have must inevitable be ungrounded at some level and it is important to identify the truths that defines that level.
3) The Super Curious embraces the prospect of never being able to satisfy curiosity. There will always be new answers to be found and sometimes even new questions to ask. In the same way we embrace the satisfaction of being able to satisfy hunger by eating every day, we accept the satisfaction to answer questions with answers over and over again.
4) The Super Curious treasure good questions and are open to trade answers for better ones in the evidence of reason or strong indications.
5) The Super Curious way of searching for truths is not to ”get there” but to ”travel in delight”. The Super Curious accepts the nomadic traits of raveling the landscape of our existential conditions. There is no end goal where we may say ”this is it”. Instead our journey through life is an ever changing inner and external environment where our basic questions continuously gets answered in new ways. Thus the Super Curious does not ask ”are we there yet”. The Super Curious always evaluates if ”where we are” and ”what we know” is a good enough place to rest at for now.
6) The Super Curious is very thorough to paint the ”white areas” of our map of knowledge. These white areas contains possible answers to ”what happen when we die”, ”What is consciousness” and ”What is the meaning of life”? By identifying that these areas are white as ”we don’t know” enables Super Curious to discuss what those answers may be without falling into the trap of arguing what is try or false.
7) The Super Curious may fill the white areas with beneficial ”truths” as long as these truths are identified as totally open-for-discussion truths. These beneficial truths may be religious ones, philosophical ones, scientifically ones or any other speculative beliefs that the Super Curious chooses to adopt. For the Super Curious it is ok to hold ”imaginary” truths to help with coping with life. This enables the Super Curious to choose the best fitting "imaginary truths”, e.g. from existing belief systems without having to buy into the "whole package”, picking the raisins from the cake if you like.
8) The Super Curious adopts the view that the truths about the future are more or less imaginary truths. Some truths about the future must be valued as more probable than others and the Super Curious strives to adopt the most beneficial answers as seen fit. The truth about the future that we will all die must be viewed as a very probably truth. But the more imaginary truth that we will be reborn must be regarded as just that. The Super Curious must identify such a truth as being located well within the white area of our knowledge web and relate to it in this way.

The whole point of being Super Curious is to start living today whilst being able to navigate using tools that may be invisible to our naked eyes and basic reasoning. Being Super Curious enables us to find better fitting truths hidden from us in our quest to find the final truth, hidden from us in our quest to ”get there”. The Super Curious realizes we are always ”there”. The Super Curious evaluates ”here”, to what degree can I enjoy my existence here, and then decides if and what to change for tomorrow. The Super Curious also understands that society is as much subject to change as we ourselves are.  Being Super Curious frees us to find tools where we thought none was to find. Further, the  Super Curious is free to live the richest possible life right away. The Super Curious does not need to find ”enlightenment” or to change the circumstances of life, i.e. trying to ”get there”, to enjoy life in the best possible way. Instead the Super Curious is free to better ask the best possible questions about the state of living and then choose the best fitting answers that makes today and tomorrow the best place possible.

The Super Curious understands that everything is never going to be ok, but it can always be better. The Super Curious understands that we don’t know the final answers to our questions, but we can find better answers. The Super Curious understands that at the core of ourselves the map is white. We may imagine there is a core there but the Super Curious understands any such answer is an imaginary truth. The Super Curious embraces the hight probability that one will never ”get there" but instead learns to love all the persons one turns out to be and in what situations those persons turns up. The Super Curious embraces change, both within ourselves and in society, as inevitable. By also embracing our insatiability the Super Curious is freed from the futile struggle with our drives and may instead turn to a nomadic travel finding ever new ways of enjoying to satisfy our needs as they arises. The Super Curios does not need to change to love oneself or to be loved by others. The Super Curious does not need to deny inevitable traits of oneself or the conditions of life but are able to face them as a source of guidance to the richest possible life. The Super Curious understands that this is a better way to use ones energy instead of waisting it to try and change circumstances we would like to have disappear all together. A better way to adress unbearable observations about ourselves or reality is to act in such a way that there negative impact on our lives are kept to a minimum costing as as little energy as possible.
