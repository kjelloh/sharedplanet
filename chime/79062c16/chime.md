> Hi Backers

> People have been asking for an update.

> The US and EU backers would have no doubt already guessed the last update saying "*So in theory we should have finally fulfilled all the Kickstarter backers by the end of June.* " didn't actually happen. Doh, sorry.

> Well, it happened for many countries that shipped from us here in Sydney, but unfortunately didn't happen for the US and EU which don't ship from Sydney.

> Also, the thing about shipping without the delayed cases didn't go ahead because we got last minute word that the cases were coming through in a few days, so it was a no-brainer to delay just another few days so everyone got them. The few days of course turned into a week, and well, anyone with experience in logistics knows the usual story...  
> Anyway, the cases are now in.

> Kane Asia (the Korean manufacturing arm) are now saying the meters for the EU backers will ship to Welectron in Germany on the 7th July. Marco at Welectron is pretty good at getting things done, so I expect them to go out ASAP after he gets them.

> Kane USA have had the units for some days now and they were supposed to have shipped them already, but I'm waiting for word back on why that hasn't happened yet. I didn't want to delay this update any longer, so I'll update again once it's happened.

> Hopefully the wheels will start turning again shortly.

> As if the case saga wasn't enough, Brymen have come up short on some remaining test leads stock (we sucked up their entire worldwide supply of leads), and there is a months delay for new stock (and Brymen are never wrong about their lead times, they must run some JIT system, because it's as regular as clockwork). Of course we won't delay some meters just because of that, so there is a chance that a few dozen people may get Kane supplied leads instead of the Brymen ones. If you do get these Kane leads and would prefer the Brymen ones, please let me know and we'll see what we can do down the track.

> Again, thanks for your patience. I do wish it was all under my control, but there are a lot of people in the chain on this one, and things just take time.
