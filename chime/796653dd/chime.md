# Todo: What about a dating site where random people meet and read the others selected novel to them?

Just saw ”Babel” on Swedish television and one of the guests suggested to a woman that had written to the show on advice to find true love, that she should find a random person and read ”Ronja Rövardotter” to her. Then out of the blue, an idea came to me for ”dating”.

1) A person assigns to the ”dating site” and states a novel he or she likes to have read for them.  
2) The dating system then sets up dates randomly, yes randomly, as the dating is for meeting all kinds of people. Not just ”the one”.  
3) Each date shall contain a moment where each attendant reads from the other attends novel. The section to read shall be where the reading stopped at the previous date.  
…

I can’t say what in this idea that appeals to me.

a) Maybe it is that the date is not about meeting another person and give something the other one wants?  
b) Or it is because the listener can take the reader in in a relaxed way without ”investigating”.  
c) Maybe it is because the randomness and the easy sharing of ”gifts” makes room for a relaxed connection where our unconsciousness can get its saying?  
d) Or…?

At least it could be easy to start this kind of service? Meetings hall always be in public spaces and the dates shall be totally random. You are not going to know the age, gender or interests of the person you meet. All you know is that this person has chosen to attend according to the rules. And you are free to share what these reasons may be?
