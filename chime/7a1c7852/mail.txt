Could we imagine a society-of-the-future to implement self-evolution using some “swarming” mechanism in the line of how Open Source Projects or C++ language evolve?

1) Consider to study C++ committee proposal and voting system as indicated at 36:30 in this talk (https://channel9.msdn.com/Events/CPP/CppCon-2015/CPPConD01V005)?

	==> Anyone may propose changes (will get an Nxxxx number somewhere in the process)?
	==> The committee discuss and votes on the form SF-F-N-A-SA (Strongly in favour, in favour, neutral, against, strongly against)

		==> Thus a vote is a kind of graph showing both both the “spread” and the “mean” of the vote (e.g. 6-7-5-2-0 leans to favours while 4-2-3-5-2 suggests no consensus)?

2) Consider the society allows “committees” to form spontaneous round subjects and gain power to impact by support through voting?

	==> How do we imagine such a mechanism to require engagement only among those that care?

3) What if we imagine each citizen to get a default manifest at birth with the option to edit the manifest at any time during life?

	==> The role of the society could then be seen as trying to enable all citizens to fulfil their manifest?
	==> The manifest could be based on “A Declaration of dependance” to impose the basic restrictions of all life sharing this planet (physically and “mentally”)?

4) Consider democratic elections are to vote for how to provide to manifest fulfilment (not what to fulfil)?

5) Consider society to have a special meta-society where the content of the default manifest is processed and decided on?

	==> Consider a goal where the default manifest should carry the evolved knowledge of what works and what not?
	==> This way the interest of the “silent mass” is protected over the progress of society?
	==> It must be ok to be a politically passive citizen and still get the benefits of the evolution of society?

6) Consider a basic mechanism is to allow experiments of societies applying different default manifests?

	==> Consider this to be a way to acknowledge that humans are poor at predict the result of our own and collective actions and beliefs?
	==> Having semi-controlled environments where new beliefs and organisation of society round those beliefs enables societies to evolve based on “survival of the fittest” mechanisms?
