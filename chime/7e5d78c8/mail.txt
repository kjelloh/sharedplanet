Try the metaphor of light beams and prisms to describe the value of questions and answers for the super curious human.

1) questions are light beams trying to illuminate truths.
2) Truths are prisms and mirrors that creates new or redirected beams as new questions.
3) Light sources are basic questions whilst split or reflected beams are follow up questions.
4) prisms and mirrors in beams of light (questions) that comes from other prisms and mirrors are grounded (follows from other truths). Those immediately after a light source are ungrounded truths.
5) light beams that continue out into the dark are unanswered.
6) there is darkness at the core and beyond the outer perimeter (unknown)

Now, the super curious aims to arrange the light sources and prisms to illuminate reality to enjoy current place and journey as best as possible.
