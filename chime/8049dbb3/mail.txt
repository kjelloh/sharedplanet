Today we know more and more about the groundlessness of our knowledge of reality. There simply seem to be few, if any, firm grounds. Sure, if we start of to trust say our senses, then we can start exploring reality using say science. But whatever we take for granted or choose to trust in the light of ”it seems evident”, they are at the end of the day always a choice that is open for questioning. So groundlessness is at the core of any body of knowledge.

What if we where to acknowledge this and start discussing how different ideologis define their groundless core. And also discuss to what degree they are aware that they are core-less and in what way they allow the empty core to be examined to make it more firm and less unknown. Maybe even allow it to change when the unknown is shown to not be what they though it to be (adress incorrect beliefs and axioms)?

The idea that all belief systems must state axioms about the unknown core and that those axioms must always be open for challenge may be a strong idea to drive society to a better state? If we could also include an idea of what constitutes a ”challenge” and what constitutes passing a challenge we may have a better tool to evolve political and religious belief systems to better suit our needs?

Blind faith may be a good thing as long as what we believe to be true has survived our best efforts to disprove it?
