We seem to live in times where things we believe to be true just grows creating more and more schisms  between competing models of reality. Do we need a new way of thinking about what is true about everything we believe to be true?

The idea here is that if we can not agree on what is "true" or how to agree on what is "true", then what can and should we do? Maybe we should find a definition of truth that fits our observation of all things we believe to be true to come up with a meta-truth?
