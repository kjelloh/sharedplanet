3) Consider how to adress the fact that Victron batteries seem to not come with built in heaters (to enable charging below freezing)?

The basic observation is that if the caravan with batteries gets cooled down below freezing there is no way to charge the batteries.

I can imagine we can run a heater of the energy in the batteries to heat the caravan and batteries to allow charging.

But this takes time and will also not work if the batteries are very low!

NOTE: The Victron chargers are described to be able to work with self-heating batteries. But this I need to confirm and also investigate how this mechanism works!
