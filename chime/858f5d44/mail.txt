2) Consider the wikipedia page on Non-transitive dice (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nontransitive_dice )?

	==> It seems the correct mathematical way to express what is not transitive is to say that the relation "X rolls a higher number than Y more than half the time" is NOT transitive on its elements (any X,Y from the set)?

"A set of dice is nontransitive if it contains three dice, A, B, and C, with the property that A rolls higher than B more than half the time, and B rolls higher than C more than half the time, but it is not true that A rolls higher than C more than half the time. In other words, a set of dice is nontransitive if the binary relation – X rolls a higher number than Y more than half the time – on its elements is not transitive."

	==> Consider to understand an example of non-transitive set of dices?

"Consider the following set of dice.

	• Die A has sides 2, 2, 4, 4, 9, 9.
	• Die B has sides 1, 1, 6, 6, 8, 8.
	• Die C has sides 3, 3, 5, 5, 7, 7."

		==> For all Dice it is a probability of 1/3 for any of its three numbers.
		==> Die A may roll 2,4 or 9
		==> Die B may roll 1,6,8
		==> Die C may roll 3,5,7

		==> Thus A will "Beat" B for 		{2,1}, {4,1}, {9,1}, {9,6}, {9,8} which is 5/9 times
		==> Thus  will "Beat" C for		{6,3}, {6,5}, {8,3}, {8,5}, {8,7} which is 5/9 times
		==> But then C will "Beat" A for 	{3,2}, {5,2}, {5,4}, {7,2}, {7,4} which is 5/9 times

		Hm... So how does B beat C while C beat A and A beats B?

		==> It seems the "opening" that allows for non-transitivity is that the relations are pairwise applicable but the pairs themselves are independent?
		==> As long as we ensures that A outcomes beats B outcomes 5 out of nine we are free to choose the outcomes quite freely?
		==> The 9 on A always beats B AND the 8 on B always beats C (counting for 3/9 "wins" A over B and B over C)
		==> But C can still beat A by having a 3, 5 and 7 that beats A 2 3/9 times and 4 2/9 times (counting for 5/9 "wins" over A)

		
		There are a chance of 5/9 that A betas B, B beats C and also C to beat A (the "beats" relation is non-transitive)
