It should be very simple although arguably not so easy to know how or what it means.

But,

1) We should do what is not bad for us.  
2) We should do what gets us by.  
3) We should do whats good for us.  
4) We should do whats best for us.

Now, can we say anything about what that means?

Well, for one, we must understand two things that serves as the back drop to these statements. The first one is the flaws of the human nature. The second is the importance of a clear vision of meaningful and rich life. The first one shall guide us to adjust actions so that we may understand and react to information and actions as intended. The second one tells us where to go and why.

We should understand that we will never get there but that the current state and direction of society shall at any given time be the best we can make it given what we currently know to be true.
