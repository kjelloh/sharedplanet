Could we propose that Kahnemans System 1 (fast thinking) is when our unconscious proposal to out consciousness is right away an acceptable action. And that System 2 is when the Unconscious and the Consciousness must interact in several steps of question-answer steps?

1) This would fit with how the unconscious processes may, over time, learn how to relate to a situation and directly propose applaudable and acceptable answer (intuition or skills like riding a bike).
2) When we need to reason it is because the unconscious is unable to propose and answer based on pattern recognition (intuition) and instead interacts with the conscious to apply a strategy to search for the most plausible answer. The ping-pong game of question-proposed answer is what we call reasoning and its purpose is to connect knowledge of patterns (truths) that we may tie together to draw conclusions on how to act.
