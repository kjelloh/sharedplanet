	==> It seems WS 81 is the analogue and WSD 81 is the digital Weller 80W soldering stations (comes with a soldering iron).

Two 80W stations, the WS 81 and WSD 81, increase the ability to work on high-mass components where a greater controlled power is required.

The analogue station WS 51 will meet most every day soldering needs.
The digital stations WSD 81, WSD 151 and WSD 161 are able to display the set, read and actual temperatures.

The WSD 161 also has dual outputs to drive 2 x WSP 80 soldering irons
