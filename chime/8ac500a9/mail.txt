What would one get if one cerated an application with the purpose to act as a personal deity to help one adress the flaws in ones life?

The idea is that perhaps a computer program can be created that through interacting with the user learns what actually works for that person to better tackle life.

This application could learn the unconscious rules we apply. Rules and behavior we are programmed with that we are not aware of but that greatly affects pour ability to strive for and feel good about our lives. Yes, this is a very loose concept but may still lead to interesting results? Could it even be that even a small set of simple variable rules of such an application can result in a quite powerful helper for us to make better decisions, feel more happier and cope with hardship in a much better way?

1) On demand it can provide ”answers” to ”prayers”? The ”Praying-interface” may be designed as some form of game where the way one play the game revelas the mood one is in and what one needs. The application then applies the rules and provides an answer to the prayer in the form of an image, an advice, as thought or just some grounding expression? The user may then play the game again and in doing so reveals in what way the rules for selecting a response needs to be changed or if the response was good enough.
2) On demand the can provide inspiration or guidance to action. Based on the rules it may try to make the user want what is good for the person. Thus, instead of just ”forcing” the person to exercise, it should find out and apply the behavior that most effectively affects the user to perform the action in the most pleasant way.

Now, this idea is very fuzzy and it borders on letting in a computer into controlling ones life instead of being in change oneself. Now, that must be avoided. The notion is rather to try and externalize some of the decision making we do unconsciously to a virtual network interacting with others. It should be viewed as augmented ”drive creator" for our own benefits. We try to take advantage of the computation power and abilities of algorithms of digital networks of computers to actually know ourselves better. Instead of letting our unconscious selves provide guidance we also let self learning algorithms be part of this input. If, at least, we still consciously reflect on our decisions we may be able to not be brainwashed into doing worse than we can without this digital deity?

3) One argument to try this out could be that we already are under the influence of these types of digital deitys, namely all these pattern analyzing algorithms operating on our searching (Google) and social interaction Facebook, Instagram) with the goal to understand how to sell us stuff. In the light of such existing deities one would argue that we also need one on our side, acting for our own benefit and not for the benefits of others.
