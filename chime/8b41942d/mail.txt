2) Consider a RGB and/or NCS encoding of a "good" green screen fabric?

It is a bit unclear from a quick web survey but using my NCS ColourPIN to NCS sRGB (close to standard RGB?) to scan my NCS Index green colours and the EncolorPedia (https://encycolorpedia.se/paints?color=5a5f34 <https://encycolorpedia.se/paints?color=5a5f34> )website I kind of get that a close enough "Green Screen" could be something like RGB #00FF00 (pure RGB green) which is something like NCS S0560-G20Y (20% yellowish green) or RGB #00b140 that seems to be named "Green Screen" which is around NCS S1070-G10Y.

Finally "Pure" NCS Green like S2060-G seems to be sRGB #009F6B which Colorpedia translates to NCS S2050-G (Close enough) :)
