	==> Consider the Specification may be compiled into Tests (e.g., for certification of the product)?
	==> Consider the Specification may be compiled into Computer Programs (The Specification defines a Software System)?

		==> The The Specification is compiled into computer code that is the compiled into a computer program (several steps of compilation and interpretation)?
