

So, the basic idea here is to share key values (properties)  of the household using IPFS and display it on an Arduino or Raspberry Pi driven touch screen.

On the dashboard we can imagine to show:

==> Invoices that needs to be paid.
==> Amounts on bank accounts
==> Known input and output payments (to know the household economy balance at a glance)
==> Food shopping list
==> Things to maintain (winter tires on car, kids to pick up, clean the house...)
...

In short - a dash board of all things to keep track of in the household to make it turn round in an effective and stress free manner.

Imagine we can interact with the dashboard though apps on smartphone and through touch screen of the dashboard display itself.
