In http://bbc.in/1I5rQOK (Science in Action ”First Results from the Philae Lander”) there is a note about some latest ants research about how ants cooperate to transport heavy stuff back to the hive. Basically there is always a majority of ants that just join in and puch the item in the same direction as everybody else. But there is always some ants, 10% of them, that act ”crazy” and just push in some arbitrary direction. Now that may seem odd. But these 10% ants actually seem to cause just enough wobbling behavior to allow scouting ants to be able to affect the general direction of the item. If all ants where perfectly cooperating in the same direction there would be no way for the scouting ants to actually affect, steer, the direction.

Now, from this one may think about how humans interact to drive society forward. Could it be that the reason we also have ”crazy” humans that does not believe in the general truths is to enable ”scouting humans” to be able to affect society progress? Like, if we did not have some uncertainty caused by humans that believe in different conspiracy ideas or new age stuff and what have you, then the society would not be open for new ideas that are actually true but sounds just as crazy?

Consider to play with this concept that we need 10% crazy people to allow scouring humans to be able to affect the majority into new ways of moving society forward!
