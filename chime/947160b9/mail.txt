Would it not be nice if we wanted that which makes us do what is good for us. We know exercise is good for health so wanting to exercise would be something to strive for. Yet we struggle with reluctance and even shear resistance when faced with many activities that we know are good for us. We find more pleasure in sitting down, relax, eat some good food and watch a movie rather than dress for exercise and endure pain and discomfort at the gym. So, could this be one way to motivate the religion of future?

1) The Religion of the Future shall make us come together and want what is good for us.
2) Society emerges from what we believe. What we believe directs us how to interact and which whom we interact. We are able to cooperate efficiently round an idea, a story, a vision of a better future. Thus, if we believe in a religion that makes us want what is good for us, this may be the idea from which a better society can emerge.
3) This idea we can call Synteocracy. We adopt the idea that we should want to do what is good for us and call this idea a created diety, a syn-theos. And then we believe this idea should direct our actions (governance = cracy) to enable society to emerge to provide our needs. This is Syntheocracy, the belief that ideas about how we can become to want what is good for us should guide our actions.
