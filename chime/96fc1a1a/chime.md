In the light of all we know we don’t know, the core-less self and core-less understanding of life (Unger calls this the groundlessness) we may need to apply evolution to the way we organize and conduct our lives.

Consider to define some process to enable ones life to evolve to a better fitted one. The idea could be to select life based on how rich it feels. Or maybe even the goal of how life should feel should also be the object of evolution. Anyhow, we from what we know of evolutionary systems we need three ingredients.

a) A selection mechanism. Based on some criteria we like in superior way of living to die out, leaving the best fitted life surviving.  
b) We need changes (random or suggested) to life to create and try out new and possibly better ones.

Now, we only have one life. So the process must be guided somehow so that we do not spend our whole life trying out lives that are all basically bad! But we should be able to benefit form others found good lives to speed up the process? With the web available we should apply social networking to exchange information about good and bad lives to evolve life faster.

Consider to start of by trying out something for my own life.

1) Make an application that acts as a ”conscious” virtual mentor that forms my life with scheduled todo activities.  
2) Evaluate a life by asking me to rate the aspects of life I believe I value.  
3) Allow some randomness to occur (or intentional input) in the proposed todo:s and the proposed aspects of life that matters (like, should I evaluate serenity or passion, mediation or creativity).  
4) Also consider to use services so that the ”mentor” may send mail, and even written messages (See http://www.postnord.se/sv/foretag/skicka/inrikes/brev/digitalt/ebrev-webb/Sidor/ebrev-webb.aspx, each physical mail seems to cost round 10 SEK?)
