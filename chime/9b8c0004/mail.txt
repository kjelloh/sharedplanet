Reading http://syntheism.org/index.php/2015/04/what-god-would-you-like-to-have-today-sirmadam/ I came to feel what a good idea it could be to create some personal gods to adress the flaws of my existence.

Quite: ”I only have two gods in my life at the moment. Treating them as sacred is the key to their success in helping me. The first is the god of silence...I have not given this god a name. Which in itself is a mnemonic as to what this god is for…The other god I have found I call “get on with it” … This god is impatient and usually rolls it’s eyes at me.”

I like these gods right away! And I come to the conclusion I should try to find the gods that I like. Gods that works although I know they do not exist.

a) Create gods that adress flaws in my life. Like a God that addresses the flaw that I have to little fun and meet to few people to have fun with. And another God that help me face the horrors of ending up in pain and hopelessness (like my father and mother?)
b) Create Gods that addresses the flaws defined by Unger(groundlessness, Mortality and Insatiability).

Oh how fun! Then I can go on writing prayers to these gods to vocalize my feelings and goals.

Oh, there no end to this play! I can also try to create a ceremony with the goal to transcend consciousness?
