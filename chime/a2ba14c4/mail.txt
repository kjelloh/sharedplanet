To mount the two vises I bought, the 14” vise requires table top thickness larger than 2 1/16” (50,8 + 1,59 mm = 52,39 mm) and the 24” vise requires a table top thickness larger than 3 7/8” (76,2 + 22,225 = 98,425 mm)

Now I can solve this by mounting the vises on underneath spacers? Because a table top thickness of around 12 cm is abut much is it not?

	==> I seems I should imagine to mount the vise jaw in some framing wood (where the wood frame mist be strong enough to allow clamping with the vise)?

The 14” vise is stated to be 17” (431,8 mm) and the 24” vise is 28 15/16” (711,2 + 381/406,4 = 711,2 + 0,94 mm = 712,14 mm)

I may be tempted to mount the 24” vise as an end vise but that would mean a workbench at least 75 cm deep. Well that’s quite reasonable is it not?

The absolute smallest (unpractically small) workbench would need to be 44 cm by 72 cm. And if we add a rim to the vise jaws of 10 mm we get the smallest bench to be 45 cm by 74 cm (rounded off to nearest cm).

We can compare with The 125 kg Sjöbergs Elite 2000 with 600 mm deep and 85 mm thick table top. My 24” vise is larger and thicker.

	==> Can we imagine a workbench where we can fill the table top with sand to add weight once it is in place. Then we could also empty it of sand when we like to move it?

	==> Don’t forget to design the wooden jaw to have wholes for bench dogs (to allow clamping of material on the workbench surface?

With the Twin Turbo Vise we can’t mount the two bench dogs so they collide with the two clamping screws! The standard vise design has the clamping screw in the middle and thus the whole for the bench dogs can go on each side. Consider a design with one central and two side mounted bench dogs?

For the Twin Turbo Vise the mechanism takes up the whole width of the jaw making it more difficult to find space for wholes for bench dogs. The bench dog wholes should ideally go all the way through the jaws? For this to be possible for the Twin Turbo Vise jaws the wholes for the bench dogs must go in front of or behind the actual clamping mechanism.

It can’t see there is any standard on bench dog dimensions but Sjöbergs bench dogs seem to be around 15..19 mm and thus they would fit in a 20 mm whole? This seems to be roughly the same dimensions (20 mm) that UK based “Benchdogs” as praised by Peter Millard on youtube (https://benchdogs.co.uk) are? I get the impression that the 20mm bench dog whole dimensions come from them being that size on Festools Multifunction Slab Tops (MFST)?

A 20 mm bench dog whole would require some additional wood to cover the whole. Say the thickness added to the vise jaw has to be at least 30 mm?
