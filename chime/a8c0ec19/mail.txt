2) Consider if we can expect there to be an "Uncertainty Principle" between knowing what is "True Now" vs "True over time" (in the same way we cant both pinpoint a particle exactly in space as well as how it moves through time as time passes)?

So the basic idea here is to view the Uncertainty Principle is physics as emerging from the fact that the measurement of location in space is one at a specific point in time while the measurement of velocity requires some time to pass to get a good reading. So we should be able to claim that the reason the precision of the location is space and precision of movement over time correlates is because in one you want to minimise the passage of time and in the other you want to maximise it. The less time you allow to pass to measure a particles location in space the more accurate position you will obtain. While the more time you allow to pass when you measure movement the more accurate measurement of velocity you will obtain.

Now, can we find a similar correlation between that which is "True now" and that which is "True over time"? Can we for example view facts about smallpox to be of the nature "True Now" while the effects of acting based on these facts as "True over Time"? If so, we should expect that the more accurate we pin point what is "True Now", the less accurate we will understand what is "True over Time"? Because to measure what is true at any infinitesimal time intervals we can't observe how that truth claim evolves as time passes. And on the other hand, the more time we allow to pass to study the outcomes of acting in faith to such facts, the less accurate we will know what is true at any specific point in time?

Well, this seems to need some though and honing to become something valuable to apply to understanding the nature of Truth. But still, my intuition is still intrigued by this proposed dichotomy between "True Now" and "True over Time". For example we may be tempted to explore if there is such a thing as "Truth Location" and "Truth Velocity"? Maybe we can try to hone an understanding of one aspect of truth to be about "where it is located" in some "space" and another aspect of truth to be about how it "moves over time".

But for this to work we need to understand a Truth be be some kind of object that can have a location in some space and thus also can move in that space? From the Harris-Petersson pod-cast debate on the nature of truth we get that it can be something about Harris arguing that truth as something "dead" (invariant to if it survives in the minds of humans or not) while Peterson took the approach that truth is something "alive" (it is true as long as it exists). If we place such a Truth-object in the Einstein space-time model of reality we get the image of harris arguing that it is enough for Truth to be objects that exists and isolated locations in space-time while Petersson argues it is not actually true if the truth object does not exist throughout the whole space-time continuum.

The interesting thing about this reasoning is that we can equally easily state that both Harris and Petersson claims that the only Truths worth talking about are those invariant to time - and thus exists throughout the space-time continuum. Then Peterson critique of Harris Truth claims is that he does not stick to his own definition. Peterson argues that something is true only if it is true throughout the space-time continuum and argues that Harris actually is satisfied if something is true only at some restricted location in space-time.

So perhaps the Harris-Petersson debate is actually about in what "space" a truth-object exists? Sam Harris tries to locate Truths "out there" in reality while Peterson insist that a Truth can only exist in the model of reality internalised by living beings? If the being dies then the Truth dies with it! And for sure, it is hard to imagine how a Truth can be anything but an internalised model of reality? It is only if we fail to realise that the reality we live in is our experience of what is "out there" that we can also imagine any truth claim about this reality to exists outside any model of said reality?
