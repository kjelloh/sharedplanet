It seems we have come to think asking for forgiveness is the highest level of mending a relation with someone we have done wrong.

What happened to second part of making-up-for-wrongdoings? The way we are prepared to try and make things up will show two things. For one, it shows that we really care for the other person. And that we like to try and heal the wounds we may have caused. But it also shows how much we repent. The more we are prepared to do to make things right, the more we feel we have done wrong. Now, asking for forgiveness does not have those components. Asking for forgiveness admits that we acknowledge that the other one thinks we have done wrong. But it fails to actually admit that we ourselves feels we have. We can still think the other one is over reacting or we may think the other one should actually understand that we dod not mean any harm. That we are without blame. Thus, following up a request for forgiveness by trying to make things up makes the effort to heal the relation again complete.
