		==> To re-capitulate

			==> Metafunctions are Templates with member type executed by compiling meta_function<Args>::type.
			==> Metafiction Classes are Class Types with template member apply executed by compiling meta_function_class::apply<Args>::type.
			==> Lamda Metafunctions are unnamed Metafunction Classes created by mpl::lamda metafunction executed by compiling mpl::lambda<meta_function>::apply<Args>::type;
			==> The apply metafunction does the same job when compiled as mpl::apply<meta_function,Args>::type.

			==> Thus, if we have a meta_function “called” with place-holders we get mpl::lamda metafunction executed by compiling mpl::lambda<meta_function<_1> >::apply<Args>::type;
