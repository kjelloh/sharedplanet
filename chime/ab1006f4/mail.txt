4) Consider to study the tools provided by Alicevision - The team behind Meshroom (https://alicevision.github.io/# <https://alicevision.github.io/#>)?

	==> It seems Alice Vision is some form of “Framework”?

AliceVision

Photogrammetric Computer Vision Framework

	• 3D Reconstruction
	• Photomodeling
	• Camera Tracking

	==> Alice Vision seems to be about Camera Tracking and 3D reconstruction?

“AliceVision is a Photogrammetric Computer Vision framework for 3D Reconstruction and Camera Tracking."

	==> It seems to be some form of Open Source, EU funded and rooted in research made by several institutions in Europe?

	==> Alice Vision seems to be about “Photogrammetry” - reconstructing 3D information from 2D images?

“Photography is the projection of a 3D scene onto a 2D plane, losing depth information. The goal of photogrammetry is to reverse this process."

	==> It seems to use two ways of 3D reconstruction - “Structure from motion” (SfM) and “Multi View Stereo” (MVS)?

“The dense modeling of the scene is the result yielded by chaining two computer vision-based pipelines:

“Structure-from-Motion” (SfM) and “Multi View Stereo” (MVS)."
