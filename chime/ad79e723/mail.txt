If all we experience are models then anything that exists in a believable model exists and there are in fact no Illusions?

So teh basic idea here is that it seems to me we are using the concept we talk about as "an Illusion" without fully understand what we mean. 

Sam Harris would say "Free Will" and "Consciousness" does not "exist", they are Illusions. He comes to this conclusion from asserting we live in a deterministic universe and thus any notions of being "free" to choose this or that or the experience of being conscious as separated from deterministic matter just can't be right.

I also just watched a discussion about wether human Male and Female Sex are "real" or are just an Illusion. And this actually made me write this Todo. Because there it was so obvious to me that a human being "a male" or "a female" is a model of reality that we care about and that it is at the core of sexual activities. The term "real" does not mean if we can map this act-in-faith model maps to say a biological model based on Genes or physiological models of the size and content of the brain. It may or may not be such a mapping. No, what we should count as real is tied to what we "care" about and how we "care" about interacting with it. Like, I get attracted to the visual and behaviour appearance of a "woman". This means I experience there is a "Woman" there. And I experience drives to interact with her in some way that differs from how I experience I care for interacting with someone that is not "a woman". Thus I find myself in a reality where I model reality in a way so that things a "care" about becomes "real" to me. "Women" exists because I care to model reality to distinguish (experience) humans that are "Women" from those that do not.

The same can be said about Sam Harris notion that "Free Will" and "Consciousness" . To me these "things" exist in my model of reality. Ic an actually go so far to say that the "fact" that for all we know I and the things I experience exist in a fully deterministic substrate of physicalism that does not change a thing about the existence of my model of reality. The physical model of reality is of no use to explain my experienced reality! It only serves as a tool for me to gain good traction (for some actions I can know with almost 100% certainty how matter will behave). But it is totally useless when I try to figure out how I should live my life.

So it seems to me that everything we can believe and trust to be useful models of our experienced reality, together in societies, does actually exists! We act as if the things in our model exists. We even manifest their existence in our physical reality. We act as-if "a Bank" exists and from that we write contracts, we engage in transactions and we build buildings in which the bank "exist". We can't say the bank, money or Sweden are "Illusions" because they become manifest through our actions. We act-in-faith to them existing. They are (perhaps only) in our model of reality. But that is the only reality we have. And because if we act as if they exist we discover that they actually do and thus they are NOT "Illusions".
