	==> It seems we can suspect that a HD (1.44 MB) 3.5" floppy disk reader can read but not write 720 kB floppy disks as these floppy disks share the same 135 track-per-inch format?

From wikipedia and other sources I get the information that a 720 kB DD 3.5" floppy disk is an 80 track 500 kB per side disk and an 1.44 MB HD 3.5" floppy disk is an 80 track 1 MB per side floppy disk (See https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_floppy_disk_formats <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_floppy_disk_formats> ). This would then mean that they share the same 135 track-per-inch density and the difference is "only" that the DD uses a lower 8717 bit-per-inch record density while the HD can sport 17 434 bits-per-inch. From this we should be able to suspect that a HD floppy disk reader should be able to pick up the recorded bits on a DD disk.

BUT - We should also suspect that a HD floppy disk driver may NOT be able to write reliably to a DD floppy disk. The magnetic substrate on the DD floppy disk is 2 µm magnetic iron oxide while on the HD disk it is 1.2 µm cobalt-doped iron oxide.

Bulk extractor for Ensoniq-formatted disk images
Brought to you by: davmp

"EnDiskEx is a command-line tool that bulk extracts instruments, sequences, songs, and banks from Ensoniq-formatted disk images (RAW, GKH, EDE, and EDA) for the EPS/ASR family of samplers. The extracted files are saved as EFE / SMF / TXT files.

EnDiskEx is designed to extract Ensoniq banks for re-creation within a different DAW. It will track down the instrument and song files from bank references even if they were saved on another disk. There also exists a disk mapping feature to correct for any errors in this process caused by having relabeled disks, forgotten to label disks, etc.

EnDiskEx has been tested and validated against a variety of RAW, GKH, EDE, and EDA images files the dev team has, including but not limited to, the ASR factory disks, the EPS 16+ factory disks, and the EPS (classic) factory disks.

Features
	• Reads images of disks formatted for Ensoniq EPS / EPS 16+ / ASR samplers.
	• Reads RAW, GKH, EDE, and EDA disk image formats.
	• Builds an in-memory library of all images you point it at (can list multiple directories or explicit files).
	• Can bulk extract all files on all images.
	• Can find all bank files, parse them, and then extract each bank to its own sub-directory.
	• Allows mapping specifications to correct for errors in bank references.
	• Extracts all Ensoniq instruments to EFE formatted files on the PC.
	• Extracts sequences/songs to type 1 SMF/MIDI files
	• Expands song steps to MIDI, overlays song tracks on top, then saves as additional SMF/MIDI file"
