To be able to finish the Blinker Brands project to brand SPC and PSB we need to design the start-up strategy to identify the most urgent deliveries.

a) SPC and SPB shall be an idea driven self-organization movement and community. This means we need artifacts that may be used to unite this movement and create and develop goals and actions.
b) The Movement and comment must scale up easily. There must be none or a minimum of costs involved. This would mean that it should rely on existing social medias and web based services that are ”free” to use. Thus, relying on a web site should be avoided. Better to use Facebook, Twitter, Youtube and Wikipedia as the base of ”home” of information and communication between actions and events within the expanding community.

Proposed way to ignite the community.

=======================================================================================================================

SPC core idea: ”Ten thousand years of rich life"

<SPC phase-1>

1) The igniting core idea for SPC is published on wikipedia as an article there (also a link to www.sharedplanetcitizen.org)
  1a) copyright is granted using a Creative Commons license. Anyone is allowed to use it as long as it is linked to core urls of the community.
2) The www.sharedplanetcitizen.org only lists references to wikipedia, youtube, facebook, twitter etc. where the core ideas are communicated.
3) An individual may support the SPC community by publishing the SPC Logo on social networks, blogs etc.
4) An individual may state oneself as an SPC by publishing an ”Declaration of Dependance” and an individual SPC-hashtag. 
  4a) Templates for and instructions on how to do this is published on free social media where this is possible. The ”Declaration” template may be published on Instagram, Flickr, Facebook as an image that one then uses as background in a word or Pages application to write the declaration.
  4b) Instructions and links to web-resources to create ones unique spc hashtag shall be published on free social medias like Wikipedia (definition of the spc hashtag), Facebook, twitter etc. The hashtag shall be a character string prefixed with spc followed by a BASE32 or BASE64 encoded GUID like ”EgAMgMP4TmOdxkGOY3coOQ” (may be generated by service like http://www.fileformat.info/tool/guid.htm). A spc hashtag may thus be #spcEgAMgMP4TmOdxkGOY3coOQ (25 + 1 character)

<SPC phase 2>

1) An app is created and made available on app-stores fro major platforms (windows, mac, iOS, Android, Windows phone etc.). The basic service of the app is to create the spc-hashtag and the spc declaration. Thus the app applies the template background and layout of the text of the declaration and produces a pdf, jpeg or other standard format for publication on social media and printing for framing on wall or what have you.

<SPC phase 3>

1) An SPC flag is designed and made free to produce using a Creative Commons license. The flag is the SPC logo on a background of the SPC and SPB colors.

Core idea: ”Ten thousand years of SPC required products and services"

<SPB phase 1>

1) The igniting core idea for SPB is published on Wikipedia as an article there (also a link to www.sharedplanetbusiness.org)
  1a) copyright is granted using a Creative Commons license. Anyone is allowed to use it as long as it is linked to core urls of the community.
2) Any business (commercial, cooperations, organizations) may aspire to be ab SPB.
  2a) http://www.businessdictionary.com/definition/business.html defines business as ”An organization or economic system where goods and services are exchanged for one another or for money. Every business requires some form of investment and enough customers to whom its output can be sold on a consistent basis in order to make a profit.
Businesses can be privately owned, not-for-profit or state-owned. An example of a corporate business is PepsiCo, while a mom-and-pop catering business is a private enterprise.”
3) A Business may aspire to support the SPB idea by publishing the SPB logo in communication or by co-branding their own brand. As long as they comply to the Creative Commons license for the SPB brand.
  3a) The SPB Creative Commons license shall state the core idea and require references to this idea when the business uses the SPB brand.
4) An SPB may state itself as an SPB by publishing an ”Declaration of Commitment” and an organization unique spy-hashtag.
  4a) Templates and instructions shall be published in the same way as corresponding SPC materials (i.e. on social medias a,d wikipedia).
  4b) the spy-hashtah shall be constructed exactly as the spc-hashtag but prefixed with spb. A spb hashtag may thus be #spb7PuEER07TLm5psav8poTvA.

<SPB phase 2>
1) An application providing similar services as the SPC-app.

<SPB phase 3>

1) Would we need an SPB-flag? This flag shall not be a ”nation flag” but rather an organization flag. A company or organisation may use such a flag to show or declare support to provide products and services are needed by the SPC ”nation”?
