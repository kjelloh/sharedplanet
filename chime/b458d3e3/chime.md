# Physis Piano

**Physis piano is an idea.**

#### The idea is to provide experienced musicians and the most demanding professionals a digital piano which is powerful, versatile, beautiful, and able to provide a sound fidelity that redefines ‘state-of-the art’.

![](http://www.viscountinstruments.com/media/wysiwyg/Sconornato-con-ombra-900x600-b.jpg)

It’s such an advanced and complete instrument that it can’t be compared with any other digital piano. It can only be compared to nature and the creative dynamics of sound itself.

An instrument like this didn’t exist before because it required technology that had not yet been developed, engineering so complex that nobody had even thought about it.

Thus, the piano which did not exist, has now been created.

Read more

#### SIMPLY THE MOST ADVANCED DIGITAL PIANO IN THE WORLD.

#### ![](http://www.viscountinstruments.com/media/wysiwyg/physis-piano-23.jpg)

####

Physis® is the name of our physical modeling technology which redefies what a 'digital piano’ can be.

This technology is based on complex mathematical algorithms and an exhaustive process – using up to 24 Billion calculations per second. The result is the ability to simulate with absolute fidelity the dynamics of the physical and acoustic phenomena of the piano. It’s a totally different approach to digital sound sampling because every single note, every chord, every slightest nuance of the performance is not the result of simple sound reproduction but it stems from an organic sound generation process which is constantly in motion, which operates in REAL-TIME, just as it occurs in the natural production of a physical instrument.  
The development of the modeling algorithms is the monumental work of more than 7 years research by a team of 20 dedicated engineers, 3 Universities and with the cooperation of international institutes.

#### THE EXPRESSIVE POWERS OF AN EXTRAORDINARY SOUND CHAIN.

#### ![](http://www.viscountinstruments.com/media/wysiwyg/physis-piano-24.jpg)

####

Besides Physis® technology, the uniqueness of the Physis® Piano comes from the amazing performance power in its technological heart. Six DSP Dual Core processors work in parallel in order to guarantee the stunning calculation power of 24 billion operations per second. This is essential to run the physical modeling algorithms in a way that generates the most accurate and authentic sound expressiveness.  
It’s easy to create your own acoustic grand piano by changing its length, its resonance, the morphology of the hammers and much more... or you can choose among 3 different “electric piano” (Clavi, Wurly, Rhody) by configuring them, for example, by positioning the transducers or defining the size of the tone bar. . Even the properties of a Mallet instrument (vibraphone, xylophone, marimba) can have its own completely customizable physical model. A rich library of high-definition sonorities and amazing sound enhancement possibilities (compression, amplification, equalization and special effects) help make the Physis piano sound chain un-equaled in the world.

#### ELEGANT DESIGN, UNIQUE TOUCH

![](http://www.viscountinstruments.com/media/wysiwyg/physis-piano-25.jpg)

The extraordinary nature of the Physis piano is not only based on its stunning technological and sound performance, but also its physical design. Simply put, there is not another digital piano on the planet with such an elegant front panel design. There are NO BUTTONS on this specially-designed multi-touch glass panel. You can even control and edit the color configuration! The high-resolution graphic display gives you easy access to visual editing – making even the most complex parameters of Physis®’s algorithms easy to understand and modify.  
In this innovative front panel, you quickly see evidence of smart and passionate design applied to every aspect of the Physis® Piano. The use of quality materials such as brushed aluminum for the piano body and real wooden piano keys with tactile “Ivory Sense” key tops can only confirm to the eyes what the musician’s ear instinctively perceives from the very first they play and listen to the Physis® Piano: this is a sensation (and reality) of experiencing an instrument which has no equal.
