I am interested in how we can make our lives more convenient and less opaque to our understanding of what is going on and how I can remain at the "helm" of my digital life.

1) Consider how I was surprised and had to perform an inconvenient operation at an inconvenient point of time to move my digital identification certificate to my new smartphone?

> *So, the basic observation here is that it was not in my awareness that buying a new phone means I must manually request a new BankID (the Swedish electronic identification framework) from my Bank.*

> *Obstacle number one is, that to be able to log in to the web interface of my bank accounts I need to log in to my banks web interface! *

> *Now, I was lucky that my old phone was still in my possession and it still works. And I could still use the BankID installed on my old phone although I had moved the SIM card to my new phone.*

> *And by logging in to my web-bank on my computer using my old phone BankID I could request a new BankID.*

> *Then on my new phone I could install the new BankID on that phone by scanning the QR code shown on the computer screen.*

> *But how is this suppose to work if I have lost my old phone?*

We detect things like nervousness as indication of untry claims. We detect inconsistency between how they behave, what they say given the context we expect them to operate in.*

> *But all these mechanisms is rendered inactive when we interact through text or service based digital interfaces. In a digital service API (application programming interface) none of the information we are exposed to in physical interactions are present. So far we have tried to replace physical information with special "secret numbers" that goes under the label of "authentication data".*

> *But in our digital life we expand our physical presence to include also our digital devices. So we have developed a system to enable us to prove that my smartphone or computer is in fact a part of my physically body. So that others can check that I am who I claim to be by checking I use a device that is a part of my physical presence.*
