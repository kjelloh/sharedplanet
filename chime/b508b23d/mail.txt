Could it be that we have confused "Truth" with the way we search for "truth"?

The main idea here is that the way we made sense of reality historically may have to be updated in modern times because the reality we have to interact with is so drastically different than it used to be?

And in fact religions has NOT been the source of truth but the guide on how to get answers as in "search for truth"?

Maybe we can even see this in the enlightenment as being a new way to search for truth, as in value ignorance as sacred and observation as confirmation.

In fact, we can choose to model any ideology, religion or truth claiming framework to be more about how to search for and reveal truth rather than a source of truth itself?

Even new atheism can be seen as more of an explanation on what truth is and how to find it rather than a source of truth itself?

In the heart of "Woke" culture is a model of the oppressed being able to observe and reveal truth that the privileged can not. So in that sense "Wokeness" can perhaps be explained more as a "search mechanism" for truth rather than a source of truth itself?
