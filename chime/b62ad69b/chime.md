Consider to design a proposal on what Darwinet services that may replace current digital bank services.

1) Darwinet must be so secure that the relation with the bank is made possible in a Darwinet domain between the customer and the bank.  
2) The bank shall be able to share data with the customer (account saldo, payment requests, transaction listings)  
3) The customer shall be able to share data with the bank (agreements, contact information)  
4) The domain hall use domain apps to provide bank services.  
5) Consider to provide Darwinet replacements fro Nordea mobile services are listed when logged in at https://internetbanken.privat.nordea.se/nsp/engine?usecase=menu&command=openmenu&guid=BBpw8SRAHDEGvWDvSoqq1wCC&commandorigin=0.leftmenu_view&fpid=MP4YaFeV8sObttlJSEqKmQCC874107811295155699xxxxxxxxxx&menuid=157&hash=iL3HQcKZbf0zFuW31lhkQACC#  
6) Delve on the problem of how to enable the relationship with the bank as automated as possible?  
7) Can we automate paying also ”paper invoices”? Like photograph them with a mobile phone and have them OCR-scanned and booked for payment through the payment service app of the bank domain? This service shall also allow this data to be shared with other apps or services to help with monetary issues (book keeping, planning, sticking to budget etc).  
8) Consider to enable the bank relation data be re-used when checking yearly declaration and tax-payment, i.e. the monetary relation with the state?  
…

The core issue is how to design Darwinet to both provide the integrity and security of the digital relationship with the bank whilst still enabling automation and sharing of information with other services of customer choosing!
