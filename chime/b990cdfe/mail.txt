Could it be an interesting measure to know how much of the Todo:s that correspond to “must” time (i.e. non voluntary time) and other activities that is required to get time to do what really feels meaningful and worth living for?

1) Consider all different types of “Must” time?

	==> Consider booked meetings as must time.
	==> Consider activities to maintain the value of owned stuff to be must time (or at least associated with “cost”, i.e. require more waged labour to enable buying a new one that was poorly maintained)?
	...

2) Consider to report time spent to keep status quo of owned stuff as a special kind of “must” time?

	==> Consider waged word required to pay mortgages?
	==> Consider waged work required to be able to buy new stuff when old stuff breaks down?
	==> Consider time required to travel to and from work as must time?
	…

3) Consider to report free time spend on enjoying owned stuff?

	==> Consider time spent using owned sailing boat?
	==> Consider time spent enjoying ones house?
	==> Consider time span enjoying ones car?
	…

4) Consider to design a way to share tips and tricks to reduce the “costs” of life to gain more of the meaningful and worthwhile time?

	==> Consider to be able to compare ones own estimates of “cost” with others?
	==> Consider to be able to just apply the Todo:s of those that seems to find a preferable way of spending time in the best way?
	==> Consider to be able to “follow” several other Todo: strategies to try them out and see how they work for one self?
	...
