1) Consider current strings lacks a layer of indirection to process their “meaning” (they only expose a raw representation interface)?

	==> a char s[40]; is just an array of bytes of type char (said nothing of what characters that are encoded)
	==> std::string s; Also said nothing about what chars are encoded.
	==> wchar_t s[4]; is just an array of two-bytes characters but sis nothing about what chars that are encoded.
	==> char* s = “Hello”; is a bit unclear regarding on how the string literal is encoded (the same encoding as the source code file)?
	==> char* s = “Välkommen”; What encoding is used here (assumably UTF-8)?

	==>  RAD Studio AnsiString s8 = “Örkelljunga”; will create an encode-aware UTF-8 string.
