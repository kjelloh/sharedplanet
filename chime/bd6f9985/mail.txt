It seems we need ideas and visions that speak a language that make it relevant in daily life of people.

1) Synetism, Religion for atheists and Religion for the future all fails to talk in a language that provides guidance to very day life. They all speka the language of philosophy, Bard and Södergård ”Synetism, Creating God in the internet age” the strongest example of this. This is not good enough. It will attract only the already enlightened if even that. What established religions has done is to mold their messages to small and easily digested chunks. You actually need to not think at all. It’s all ready to apply to the daily life. So, what can we do to ignite the religion off the future so that it may serve as an inspiring and readily acceptable framework of guidance to help citizens reshape the world into the future? For sure a kind of revolution is required. And a revolution needs a very simple and true message to take of. Could the super-curious human be such a message?
2) The religion of the future must begin by telling the story of why it exists, what reality it is there to adress. I propose it shall adress the three crisis that is seemingly converging to explode all at once. 1) We are running out of space and resources to provide for humanity (we fail to conduct our lives in a sustainable way). 2) Society is failing to provide for the needs of the citizens (the incitements of production is making the need for paid labour to vanish) and 3) Science is failing to make sense of the findings about reality (Quantum physics, human scale physics, and cosmology keep coming up with new findings that are failing to make sense when brought together).
3) The news now obsessively report of the need to protect nations and individuals from being threatened of refugees on the run in need of better lives. The reaction is to close borders, fight for self governance (IS one bloody example) and flight from unbearable conditions. At least the super-curios human may be able to realize the system is failing and the solution is to change society to its cores to meet the needs of life.
