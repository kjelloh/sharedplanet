Commercials are basically messages to us that we are not to be content with what we have or how we feel about our current life. Some even propose the most efficient commercials play on our fears. The fear of now being loved. The fear of not belonging. The fear of being left out. The fear that we are bad parents, that we do not protect our children enough and so on.

Regardless, if our goal is to be happy we should avoid commercials the best we can. And those who now claim that we can choose not to be affected by commercials should just think again. It is very easy to show that commercials on the contrary are designed to affect us, especially on that level of unconsciousness. We like to think we are logical beings able to make rational choices. Nothing could be further away from the truth. In reality we do what we want to do and rationalize to quite an extent when needed to still feel in charge.

1) If commercials was not a central part of capitalism we would not allow it in public spaces and might even restrict it.
2) Commercial belittle us and asks us to conform rather than explore.
3) Commercials manifest the beliefs of current neoliberal democratic capitalism and hides truths about its flaws and inabilities to provide for our true needs.
4) Commercials drives shallow change to create consumption and prohibit deep change that could transform social organisation.
…

Consider to touch on the complexity of trying to get rid of commercial messages at the core of our society.

a) Without commercial the free market can’t work.
b) Without a free market development can’t happen.
c) Without development there can be no growth of economy.
d) If the economy does not grow the market for labor will shrink away.
e) If the market for labour shrinks unemployment will raise.
f) If unemployment raises more and more will get poor.
g) If the number of poor grows the society welfare will be unable to provide.
h) End result - society will collapse and we end up being poor and without purpose in life.
