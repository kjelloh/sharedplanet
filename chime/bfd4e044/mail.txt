	==> It seems macOS may be configured to use a BASH shell (older versions) or a ZSH shell (newer versions)

And each shell has its own way of how the $PATH variable is assembled when a shell is created.

Just google and be dismayed over how many ways there seem to be to ensure the correct PATH variable is instantiated!
