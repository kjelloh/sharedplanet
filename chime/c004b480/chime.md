The only constant we know of is change. Nad things change very rapidly now. Thus Society 2.0 can’t rely on what we currently know but rather on how Society should relate to new knowledge and how to apply it to best serve the goals of society.

In this way, Society 2.0 must be pragmatic to tools, rules, what is right and wrong and so on when it comes to actual actions. It can’t argue from an absolute point of view that something is the correct way of doming something as new discoveries or ideas may suggest otherwise. To best embrace change to the advantage of the society a key component must be to keep searching, both for the right tools but also for the right goals. This basic acceptance of groundlessness should in the light of change be the best approach possible?
