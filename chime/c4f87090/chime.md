==&gt; The Swedish term would then be "Synilikrinakrati" (quite a mouthful)?

==&gt; The term could be argued to mean "The ruling "-cracy" of the created truth "Syn- Ilikrina-""

==&gt; Could we imagine to shorten the "Ilikrinia" to "likri-" (Getting Synilikricarcy) or even just "Ili-" (getting Synilicracy)?

==&gt; The greek word "alíthia" is defined to mean "truth" in a broader sense (including veracity, as in truthfulness, that which is true, accuracy and correctness)

==&gt; The Swedish word would then be "Synalitiakrati"?

==&gt; The term could be argued to mean "The ruling" -cracy of the created "what is accurate, correct and true"?
