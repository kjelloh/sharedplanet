It seems to me the debate about for or against religion or science is in fact the question of what model or reality we should act in faith to and how such a model can and should be expressed and understood?

> I am highly unsatisfied with how my fellow humans choose to interact with reality! Let me try to explain and give some examples.

> *I started this day by watching some youtube videos that made me upset, sad and curious. For one the Epstein story hit me unaware what was going on. I clicked on the Jimmy Dore show story on Epstein and was very dismayed with the tone and glee in this episode. I did not know anything about Epstein, what he had done or what the fuss was all about. So to me the way Jimmy Dore with a smirk on his face commented on Epsteins suicide was just appalling! For Gods sake, a man seemed to have committed suicide and Jimmu Dore seemed to have nothing to say about this than finding something about it humorous?! This is just NOT the way I want humanity to interact with reality or put another way - this is NOT how I want humans to treat each other or narrate what has happened and how to feel about it. A suicide is a suicide! Surely this is a loos loos situation however you look at it? A sad history about human shortcomings and hurtful drives for violent action?*

> *So I watched another youtube video with the title "Jeffrey Epstein Accuser Shares Story Of Alleged Rape For 1st Tim" to try and get some grip of who this Epstein guy was and what the story behind the scenes was all about. And found myself listening to a woman who explained how Epstein had interacted with her in such a way that it ended up with sexual intercourse against her will. And I got dismayed again! This just did not go down well with me. Surely no one thinks what Epstein did to this woman against her will was a good thing? But somehow the interview was framed to suggest that Epstein did? Again, I felt sad and dismayed with this way of interacting with reality. To me there just is something wrong with the model of reality and how humans work and the rules and responsibility of human interactions that are just wrong? Surely we should assume that when we end up hurting each other it is not on purpose. Surely we should not frame reality to explain hurtful actions as being done with hurtful aims? Or am I just naive? At least I get upset with this model of reality. I find it unsatisfying to impose an explanation of bad outcomes as being the result of rational and planned actions by powerful people. This just must be an inadequate model of human drives?*

> *I then found the video ""THE EPSTEIN CONSPIRACY" and now I was faced with a surrealistic experience. The host who calls his channel SomeBlackGuy was playing some MOD, which seems to be a game where the main character has been replaced with a character from some other context (in this case Shrek from the movie Shrek), where he as first shooter very splatterly killed off zombies while talking about the Epstein Conspiracy. I only watched a few minutes but the whole thing made me feel sad and creepily un-in-touch with reality. Again, here a man had committed suicide after having being confronted with and convicted for enforcing himself on underaged women. And this guy was commenting on it from the view of conspiracy and analysing how other commentators and conspiracy mongers treated Epsteins suicide. So much blood! So virtual! So unreal! Is this how we are supposed to model reality these days? I can see that we do. But I feel this just is an inadequate way of modelling reality to guide our actions?*

> *So I switched track, or so I thought, and watched youtube channel Rationality Rules video "Jordan Peterson vs. Sam Harris (Vancouver, 1): Analysed Arguments" and found myself again thinking the model of reality that this guy was arguing within also was inadequate. The discussion between Jordan B Peterson and Sam Harris is a hot one, an interesting one, but somehow the core thing that is really interesting about these conversations seems to go uncommented. We need a model of reality to use to navigate reality with. And this model must somehow be true to the way humans operate in reality. And the model behind the scenes, provided by Sam Harris and the host for Rationality Rules just does not fly with me. It is just so naively true but in the same way so obviously inadequate for any human to navigate day to day life? Of course religion has gotten reality wrong. Of course we have made great progress by having built a very good and effective model of reality that enables us to use logic in our reasoning to build machines and stop killing each other for believing the wrong things or abiding to the wrong good. But the elephant in the room is that from this does not follow that the practices or narratives of christianity is still not a good model of the human reality. It may be an old model for sure. The christian model of reality in the form of narratives on how to interact with this reality may be outdated and to a large part hard to relate to for humans living in the modern world. And for sure many clear rules about what you are allowed to do (say take slaves) or what you are not allowed to do (say go to bed with someone of the same sex) is not longer adequate or how we want to be able to express ourselves today. But that does not mean we do not need a model of reality that makes sense to us when dealing with complex situations regarding human interactions. To a large part we do not interact with material things as "dead" things obeying physical laws. We interact with a material world built by humans driven by human beliefs of what a good material world should be. And whatever we may think about it we are at the core driven by what we care about things. We act to transform reality to become what it ought to be. Our physical model of reality enables us to manufacture things. But the things that actually exists and the way we care for them existing, actually is a very real part of the reality we live and in is not fully explained by physical laws. To know what education I should get, what partner I should marry and what things we should buy for our common life and possibly give to our children, should we get some, we need a model of reality that is much larger than the physical model of the world. To reason about how reality ought to be, we need a model that enables us to draw conclusions about right and wrong actions in a self organising system of humans, each being an actor that cares about what you and I are doing and what the outcome of these actions seems to be. This is not a model based on cause and effect. It must by definition be a model that explains what will emerge from what we believe exists and what we believe should exist.*

I also felt a longing for a model of reality that made sense to me and that I would like humans to act in faith to. *
