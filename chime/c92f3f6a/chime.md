My Brother HL-L2375DW laser printer suddenly refused to print with the error "Low Toner..." but I wanted to force it to really empty the cartridge!

1) It seems this trick to "Reset" the Low-Toner block worked for me (<https://www.inkhub.com.au/brother-tn-2450-toner-reset> )?

==&gt; For me it worked to select "Continue" in step 3 and then hit &lt;Go&gt; (and then wait around one minute!)

  
> #### HL-L2375DW / HL-L2350DW / HL L2395DW

> 1. Press the Up or Down arrow key to choose General Setup, and then press OK.

> 2. Press the Up or Down arrow key to choose Replace Toner, and then press OK.

> 3.Press the Up or Down arrow key to choose Continue or Stop.

> 4. Press Go.



*Note: I had to wait for around a minute or two for the printer to start printing again. It may or may not have been a coincident?*
