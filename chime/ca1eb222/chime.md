The Super Curious may treasure the feeling of wonder by keeping the door open to better truths. In the unknown lies the ability to feel wonder. It is magically wonderful that we exist, that the world exist, that I am able to interact with other living creators and the world, that I have the ability to feel. All this wonder comes from the vertigo created when faced with the fact that we have no clue to why all this is possible. And by keep searching and trying to find deeper. more meaningful and more beneficial answers to these questions we may continue to feel wonder for ever. If we should, by some miracle, find the answer tomorrow, then we might loose the ability to wonder? Thus, we shall enjoy the prospect of never being able to find the final answer and in the same time embrace a future never robbed of reasons to feel wonder.

1) The Super Curious must thus find the dialectic between wanting to find the answer whilst also emotionally experience life. The Super Curious probes life with mind and emotions.
