The Super Curious human understands that each society emergence in relation to previous and coming ones are better in some sense and worse in others. And any ”better than” function we try to apply may turn out not to be transitive.

1) We shall always strive to ”better” our experienced existence but we must always understand that any new state is just a new state, it may or may not be a ”better” state. As a nomad may choose to stay at one place in the summer and in another in winter, we as a society may choose one way to organize society during a period and then go for another order the next period deepening on inner and outer environment properties.  
2) There is no end state. We are always faced with the questions of in what qualities current state if a good one and in what qualities we may find a better state.  
3) We must understand that two steps of worsening society may take us to a better state and the opposite. We must understand that ”Progress” is just an illusion until we define how we evaluate ”better than” we musty allow different such definitions to co-exist to be able to use the inevitable change to our advantage. How does individualism, capitalism and liberalism work for us? How does socialism, welfare state and solidarity work for us? What may in the light of what we know and believe can be defined to be "better than” the society we have today?

The Super Curious should understand that as everything changes the future is open and any truth about how to better things will with a low or high probability be wrong. Thus the Super Curious proposes we need to be able to try out candidates for better societies, adopt the ones that seem to work and continue to do so for ever. We may argue that we should try to find the most sustainable way of society but not even that can be stated as an absolute fact. The nomad is always open to move when the conditions change so much that it seems like a good idea.
