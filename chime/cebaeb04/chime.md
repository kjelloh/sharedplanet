Listening to Hubert Dreyfus talking in swedish ”Filosofiska rummet” (http://t.sr.se/1Vi5qmK) it seems Dreyfus argumentation may be used to invent practices to be described in the bible for the Religion of the Future.

1) propose practices to create meaning in life. These practices shall for one promote how important the feeling of meaningfulness is, that there is no absolute meaning bait that we are wired to feel activities or states meaningful. So we may propose practice that helps us experience meaningfulness.  
2) Consider to propose self-imposed restrictions in life to experience meaningfulness. The reasoning is that restrictions creates the possibility to do right or wrong which is a base of feeling meaningfulness. The freedom to think or act in any way one likes robs us of the ability to archive something that requires an effort which is in itself a feeling of reward that can give meaningfulness to life.  
2a) One may try to not throw any food and experience how that makes one feel about life? It can be argued that not throwing food is logically a good thing. But does it also create a feeling of meaningfulness that we can’t experience in a freedom to consume or treat food any way wee like?  
2b) One may try for defined periods to spend as little money as possible.  
2c) One may try for a period of time to give to beggars.  
2d) One may try for a period of time to not use the car.  
2e) One may try for a period of time to not watch any television.  
2f) One may try for a period of time to not watch the news.  
….  
3) Consider to define a way to propose practices in a collaborative form, e.g. social media? Consider a Darwinet App?
