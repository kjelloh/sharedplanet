We live in an era where we use money to make people work. And money is used to spread wealth. Thus wealth to all requires work for all. But what if we want to reduce work we do not like to do? And how shall we tackle the economic incitements (enhancing of profits) that slowly drives paid work extinct?

One way would be to focus on one aspect of a rich life, namely meaningfulness. If society was to focus on providing meaningful lives to its citizens, then meaningful work would be the reward instead of money. We would not need money to incite work that needs to be done. And work that is hard and not meaningful we are free to automatize and hand over to computers to do.

That we are able to work for others without getting paid are known. This is the drive for Wikipedia, Open Source projects, voluntary projects of all kinds, the arts and so on. One may even argue that if poverty was eradicated the single strongest motivation to do anything would be meaningfulness? 

Could these be the model of a future society?
