We tend to think about economic systems as something that are governed by rules in the same way as processes in nature. Now this can’t be true as economic systems emerges as the result of humans engaging in transactions and humans are not acting according to fixed rules as nature is. Or are we?

Thomas Piketty in his book ”Capital in the twenty first century” studies statistical models of what has been going on in the world economy the last 1000 years or so. Now, the value of this study should be determined on how stable the rules from which the system emerges are? He seems to touch on some of these when he mentions that government policies in the form of regulations may greatly affect economic systems behavior. Would be interesting if he also talked about how we, as humans, are ruled by what we believe in and how our drives makes us behave and decide to act.

So, it should be valuable to ask the question to what degree the behavior of economic systems actually reflect who we, as human begins, are. And in that case, to what degree and for what reasons these behaviors may change when what drives us human changes.

1) Our genes ”wants” to thrive and thus we have properties that makes us act to make this happen. One would then suppose that our relation to money, the value of money and what you can do with them once you get them may be influenced of the goal of our genes? Now genes can mute (takes long time) and cross-mix (slow but faster than mutation) so gene change affects on economic systems should be slow compared to other effects?  
2) Our memes compete for expo nation and acceptance. Memes like individualism, the right to own and trade and priority of freedom to choose to great extent should affect how economic marktets behave and evolve. For sure, even Money in itself is a meme based on the assumption that everything has a value that may be compared on the same scale of value, i.e. an amount of money.  
3) Laws and rules regulating how transactions is allowed to be made and protection of the framework of owning capital and trading goods and services are man made and may or may not be affected by the drives created by genes and memes? At the very least, these rules and laws may change quite rapidly if wanted or needed.

So, in the light of us wanting the emerging society and in this case the economic system emerge as the ”best” one to provide for our short and long-term needs, to what degree is that possible. How much may we change our behavior and the emergent behavior just by molding what ideas about conduct and goals we hold true? IS it, for example, possible to change our ideas of value and have say different currencies for different types of values?
