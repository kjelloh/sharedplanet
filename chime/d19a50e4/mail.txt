I am searching for an understanding of life that carries value and can be expressed in a few words.

Maybe we can start with the observation that the thing that is alive may be seen as a piece of reality separated from the rest of reality? And then we can say that this alive thing has to internalise a model of reality and so be able to be “about” reality. This finally tempts us to say that this living actor is most occupied with interacting with reality in a repeatable way? Every interaction with reality that leaves the living actor existing means it is about reality in a good enough way.
