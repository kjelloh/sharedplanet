Maybe it is a good way of understanding Unger to try and condense his message?

1) He seem to start of with identifying three irreparable flaws in the human nature.  
a) We will Die (page 1)  
b) Groundless knowledge of the world (page 5)  
c) Our desires are insatiable (page 17)

==&gt; Conclusion - Everything will never be right (page 35).

2) He singles out ”Belittlement” is NOT being an irreparable flaw in human nature (page 25)  
”To deny the inescapable features of existence - death, groundlessness and insatiability - is to commit … an insult to ourselves. … We seize upon devices and stratagems that divide and enslave is under the pretext of empowering us.”

==&gt; Conclusion - We must search a society that minimize our belittlement?

3) He identifies three evolutionary steps (revolutions) in human orientation to life  
a) Overcoming the world  
b) Humanizing the world  
c) Struggle with the world

==&gt; Conclusion - Time for a new orientation to life? - The Religion of the Future.
