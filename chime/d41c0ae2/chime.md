Through the app one may vote for, provide and select to follow someone else's choices in life.

1) use the same phone  
2) eat the same food  
3) use the same types of pens (e.g. Fountain pen)  
4) shave with the same tools (e.g. Wet shaving using same brand)  
...

The goal is to evolve ways of living where you trade the freedom to choose every little aspect of your life for freedom to have more time just enjoying life. Also, this enables evolution of more sustainable way of living by adopting choices that is collaboratively evaluated and developed.
