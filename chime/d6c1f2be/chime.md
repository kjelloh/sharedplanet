Even when there is not enough to do for everyone to get a pay check the economical system must ensure production of food and shelter to everyone.

1) We can't have an economy that does not allow paid labour to go away. The very essence of development is to get rid of labour. That's why we have kept inventing since we tamed fire. And that's still in the core of capitalism. Problem is we still hasn't found a way to allow this to actually mean freedom from labour.  
2) We should design the economical system to provide to our needs also when the actual labour needed for this gets reduced.  
3) The solution must also solve the problem of how to share the burden of labour we still have to do. And also how we are to experience meaningful lives if labour is not asked from us. The latter being perhaps the most novel and unknown to us? At least on the global scale.  
4) the solution shall allow for citizens to engage in work not required by the monetary economy. It is allowance for a shrinking market for paid work we shall address. We may need a way to distinguish between labour and non-monetary dependent activities so they may co-exist as independently as possible.fir example, labour one shall do in return for money whilst non-monetary related activities must totally voluntary. At no time must there be incitements to force people to do labour as non-monetary related activity?
