The number one priority of our contemporary times afar freedom. And freedom means freedom to choose. Freedom not to have to constrain our choices.

This also shows in organisations and the way we like to do things. We instinctively react if someone sais we must do something in a special way. In the workplace we like to be able to choose for ourselves what is the best way of performing our tasks. And computer systems are often very bad to provide these choices to us. Instead they require us to fill in forms and press buttons in very rigid ways.

But we then forget that restrictions may also mean support. A broken leg or wonder knee may need fixation to heal. An athlete may excel when finding the exact way to perform not deviating even the slightest from the mot efficient way of moving. And not having to choose is also freedom. Not having to think when thinking has already ben sufficiently done. And if we can’t allow some ways of doing things to be lesser good we will not be able to develop.

More freedom simply means less efficiency. More freedom means less progress. It is impossible to be both free and efficient in the same time. Creative maybe, but not efficient. To be efficient means constraining your choices and effort from being wasted on that which has been proven to be less sufficient. Efficiency means to find a way to fix a problem once and see to it that it stays fixed, i.e. applying restrictions on how it is done.

With computers we are able to automate tasks. Automation means restrictions in what we manually do. You trust a machine to do boring stuff so that you may focus on being creative. But this also means you must allow yourself to trust the computer to not malfunction or create errors. What a computer does is out of your control. If the computer fails you may be stranded. When faced with this kind of choices one must still remember to opt for efficiency. Only if efficiency in work fails should one accept freedom as the inferior choice. Because freedom in work tasks means more choice and more wasted energy to solve the same problem over and over again. Efficiency in work means more time and energy left for creativity, attending to new and better ways to perform the task. Efficiency means the work may become more meaningful as we are then free to put our attention to stuff that still may need more thinking.
