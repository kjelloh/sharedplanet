2) I got a confirmation e-mail ok.

4) Confusion! What just happened?

?: Why was the payment declined?  
?: Why was the mail sent to the wrong e-mail address?  
?: Why was the invoice information wrong?

==&gt; The links provided to the order did not allow me to cancel it.

?: What did this “link to Apple ID” mean anyway?

==&gt; I discovered Apple Support don’t answer the phone until Monday.

==&gt; They don’t open until 9 AM.  
==&gt; But the did not tell me that, until I had gone through the automatic answering machine to select my business and order number!!

==&gt; My order had been cancelled

==&gt; But I still did not know what caused Apple pay to be wrongly configured in the first place?

==&gt; But the did not tell me what card was affected (and I forgot to ask)

18) At 12:00 PM I now wait for order cancelation conformation e-mail.

Conclusion:

1) Apple Pay is separate from any Apple ID account  
2) The Apple Pay information is stored in the phone (NOT in the Apple ID account)
