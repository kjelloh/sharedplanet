-1) Consider to write an abstract of what he is teaching?

> It seems he suggests we should pair Vipassana ("Insight") with an existence mode reached by opening up and create a "loving" relationship with yourself, someone near, someone neutral, someone your are in conflict with and then all humans and possibly all living (layers of opening up and transform existence mode in layers of wider and wider relationships).

> The traditional mantra in Metta is "May X be happy and healthy", "May I experience X true suchness".

> John Vervaeke suggests a more modern mantra "May I see the Moreness in X"

> Through your mantra you are trying to change you from a having mode into a being mode in relationship with yourself and others.

> First become aware and then allow trasnformation (your model of the other and interface for existing in relationship with them)
