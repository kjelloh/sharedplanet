Consider the downsides of current individual freedom and competitive market idiom that creates service-centric optimisation instead of divid-centric ones?

1) Could we reason that competition will lead to identity fragmentation?

	==> If services compete to provide unique functionality - then users will use more services and thus need to create more digital identities?

2) Consider to define divid-centric services as a term to use to define what traits we would like services to have (and that competing-for-profit is less likely to create)?

	==> Services are dividual-centril if a dividual is on control and owns the result of used services?
	==> Facebook is not dividual-centric in that user data is stored (and in some respects “owned” by Facebook.
	==> Dividual-centric services enables and allows the user to re-use the result of one service in another.
		==> Such re-use may be provided by a third divid-centric service.
		==> Services that allow Facebook user-data in twitter is not divid-centric (data is still stored, owned and controlled by Facebook and twitter)


3) What incitements can we imagine to drive emergence of dividual centric service models?

	==> If the divid owns her data she will be able to get paid by services to share the value of that data with the service?
		==> If a divids social network was not in the Facebook service itself (a bi-product of using the service) then the divid could sell access to it to allow Facebook to monetise on this information?
		==> A serverless P2P social network could provide such an option to the user (no need to pay Facebook with user data to get a social network service)?

	==> The society could drive for society services to be divid-centric (society is there to provide for divids)?

		==> Then society must reduce using market incitements to provide social services?
		==> Civil funding may be sued to develop divid-centric services (e.g. an automatic tax declaration service)?

			==> Note how a tax declaration service must be divid centric in that it must be able to access all tax-related earning and spending of the dividual.
			==> And that information may today be stored with each business relation and transactions performed by the divid with different providers?
…
