The four winners of Region East all seem interesting to follow up on for several reasons.

1) MindCap seem to want to address the growing problem of over-stressed and discontent citizens in current workspaces and social climate? But do they not miss out on how we really work (emdividuality) and that it is the environment we have cerated (society) that is “sick” and not ourselves?
2) Conscious Fashion Labs want to drive circler economy through fashion? Is this a SharedPlanetBusiness and something for a SharedPlanetCitizen to support? If so, should we contact them to design hip SPC-garments and accessories?
3) DIB Services seem to propose a monetary incitement to regulate competitive markets to not get over-populated? Is this something for SPB to adopt and support? Or is there an idea in here about the Economy of the Future?
…

Consider to follow up on these ideas and relate them to the models of Emdividuality, The religion of the future, SPB and the Super Curious?
