

So, the basic idea is that if the current economy incentivise us to behave like mould that makes food rot, then we can imagine we should in fact create an economy so we behave more "intelligently" like the Mycetozoa (the slime mould).

The inspiration for this metaphor is that, yes, it seems humans collectively explore and exhaust resources very much like ordinary mould consumes food until nothing is left.

The Slime Mould on the other hand is studied because it expresses a kind of "intelligent" behaviour in that it can self organise in a growth process to search and choose what to eat, could model a better way for humanity to collectively behave.

So, can we imagine an economic system that distribute currency to make humanity as a collective to NOT exhaust our resources and "search" for a sustainable life for humanity?
