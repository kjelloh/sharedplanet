Consider I can know things abut myself from some observations on what I do and how I feel about my life and where it is going?

I fell pain when I have to take action to try and change reality. If I want to build a shelf out of wood I get very stressed out when I actually have to cut the wood, make the measures os even just buy the materials. It is as if I question my choice and fear that the money I spent and the energy I put into actually making the wooden shelf materialise will be in vain. That I will fail and the whole endeavour will turn out to be in vain. In contrast I find it very enjoyable to design the shelf. To investigate what materials are out there and how I can use those materials to make the shelf. I can spend hours making drawings of the shelves on paper or in a CAD program. But when it comes to actually put the foot down and actually act-out the “truth” I have decided for the manifestation of actual wooden shelves, then I feel anxiety and get stressed. Why is this? What conclusion should I draw from this observation. How should I relate to these traits of my personality? or rather, the traits of me that emerge when I interact with my perceived reality?

I easily get obsessed with tasks that involves thinking and dreaming. I really love to build abstractions in my head. What is true? How can I make this better? How does this even work? What is out there? How can I build stuff? Have can I invent software that does something important in a better way? How can I make change happen? My head is constantly focused on finding and answering questions. The process of asking new and interesting questions and the quest to answer them provides the greater joys to me! And I can get so occupied that I forget to eat, to pay the bills or clean the house. Having to drag myself from the activity of investigating something; writing down my findings; designing things; stating propositions and going down rabbit holes of never ending files of Why:s, is very painful to the limit of impossible. It seems to all come down to my experience of meaning. It feels meaningful to investigate and research. And it feels totally meaningless to have to maintain that which already works. Having to repeatedly clean my house when I already cleaned it just a week ago feels almost like an insult from reality. Why is the reality such that my house gets filled with filth when I leave it to do its own thing? Why do I repeatedly spend time to restore order when order had already been restored?

I would like to share my thinking with others. Not so much to get feed back as to share the joy of thinking the things I think. I would like to have company in my quest for interesting questions and intriguing new way of answering them. I like to have people to play this game with. But I can’t honestly say I want interaction. I want to be with people that share my interests. But it seems I do not want to provide to other peoples needs? I really don’t like when people ask me questions and expect me to provide a solution to a problem. I can prove information about observations I have made and conclusions I have drawn. But I do not want to tell people what to do ow how to solve their problems. Is there a contradiction here? For a long time I thought there was. But I don’t think so anymore. I like to provide answers to what-is. I do not like to tell people what the ought. I feel pain when I tell people what they ought to do! And as a result I can actually despise people that lean on me for such information. I really do not like people to lean on me! Leave me alone! I actually feel anger when people does not take responsibility for their own oughts. When they are not honest about their own short-comings or the hard-ship of finding their oughts from their is.
