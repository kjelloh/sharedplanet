	==> Consider to use Apple [mini display port] Thunderbolt 2 <---> Thunderbolt 3 [USB-C] BUT remember the shortcomings:

		1) The Device MUST be a PCIe device (No USB protocol can travel the Thunderbolt 2 in to the Mac)
		2) The Device MUST have its own power (No power is transferred through the Apple [mini display port] Thunderbolt 2 <---> Thunderbolt 3 [USB-C] cable)!

	==> Apples [mini display port] Thunderbolt 2 <---> Thunderbolt 3 [USB-C] will tunnel PCIe (NVMe) or Display Port (NOT USB as Thunderbolt 2 does NOT support tunnelling of USB)
