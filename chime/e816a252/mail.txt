The notion that time is like a dimension is something I like to challenge and thinking about time as a kind of "cost" is one way I like to explore.

So the main idea here is to model time is a kind of money used by reality to "pay" for transformation of the state of "now".  But just as money, once you have spend some time you can not get it back. You may use "new" time to try and reverse what you had happen with some time you spent. But you can not reverse the actual spending of time.

So time is no more a dimension than  money is. You can not travel in the dimension of money. There is no way you can spend money to move forward in "money" to see what it is like "there". And you can not "unspend" money to go back to how it was before that money was spent.
