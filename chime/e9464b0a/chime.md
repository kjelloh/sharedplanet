What would happen if we decided each citizen must choose X friends (say ten) and over a year their income ration must not deviate more than Y between highest and lowest income among the friends?

1) The idea is to create a global triple down effect by connecting all humanity into this friends network.  
2) The rules for friendship must ensure no human are more than say 7 friend steps apart from any other human! 7 Steps allows then maximum income ratio to be 7^2 = 128 times.  
3) The model does not require an authority to supervise it. It can be created by a global peer-to-peer network with some app with the purpose to upheld the friend rules and calculate monetary transfers.  
4) The model can start of as voluntary and have the X and Y values to be configured to a bearable impact until the network is large enough? The network can implement some auto-configuration to allow members to be eased in from the beginning. For example, in the beginning the network would require no more than a 1% up or down adjustment of incomes.  
5) Question is, what can be the long term incitements for those with high income, those who will get their income lowered, to join the network? Can we motivate this with i higher Deep Richness? If so, how can Deep Richness be ”paid” to those who contribute monetary?

8) With nine earning a similar amount and the tenth earning nothing, then the cost of the tenth is divided bearable on the nine that har an income. Now on a global scale this is not a possible distribution.

So over time the one that originally was alone paying will not over time be alone and perhaps quite rapidly come back to his original earnings?  
10) The network can be rigged to require production and consumption to also be within the limit of the planet?
