When it comes to weather we do not just report what the weather has been doing and expecting the audience to draw its own conclusion about what the weather is going to be the coming days.

But when it comes to news that is exactly what we do. We select events over the world but we do not try to provide some predictions on what this is going to mean in the near future.

Why not try to report the news in the form of forecasts? A news channel could for example provide some different forecasts and then also follow what forecast that turn out to be the best predictions. Those with good predictions should of course be trusted more in the future (just as yr.no is somewhat more strutted than swedish SMHI when it comes to weather forecasts).

When aiming for forecasts we are also able to discuss what we want to forecast. Aiming our forecasts could probably enable us focus on what may actually affect our lives and diminish the value of reporting about small tragedies or scandals that does not relate to the important trends.

1) Show ongoing violent conflicts and forecast how the conflicts may evolve and who may be affected. We should try to integrate all ongoing wars and order them in number of causalities, number of affected civilians and affect on the world as a whole. Reporting should involve history so that we know more than just the world wars or the close wars. 2) Show ongoing refugee movements world wide. Where are people going and why. What affects the streams? What regions are affected and how?  
3) Put world economy into perspective. Who owns what and how does ownership change. What happen with income and distribution of wealth. How does that affect different areas. What are the forecast for me?  
4) Report about environmental change and why. What are the forecasts? What is up, what is in decline?

6) make forecasts about the life for me and different areas in the world on month, year, decade and century scale. Especially make forecast what to expect in old age.
