Synteocracy (ruling by the created god) is an idea that society emerges from the ideas we choose to believe. Thus, by caring for what ideas we put faith in we can make the preferable society emerge.

Compare with current society we like to believe we have rationally constructed whilst it to is the emergence induced by the current held beliefs in individual freedom and capitalistic market economy. The construction of society, the institutions we build and the organisations we create are all the result of striving to comply to and protect these ideas. If we choose other ideals we would get a very different society.

1) Consider Ungers deep freedom and deep equality.  
2) What about Högdals ideal of the super-curious citizen?  
3) What impact can Shared Planet Citizen have?  
4) What about the idea to aim for meaningful lives instead of productive ones?  
….

Synteocracy could become the name of this idea to search for the incitements in the form of beliefs and ideas to drive emergence of the society best suited to sustainably provide for our needs?
