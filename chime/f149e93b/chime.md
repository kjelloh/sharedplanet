In TED talk ”What makes us feel good about our work?” (http://www.ted.com/talks/dan_ariely_what_makes_us_feel_good_about_our_work) economist Dan Ariely reports on his findings about what motivates us to work.

1) Dan Ariely seems to draw the conclusion that even small feeling of meaning (although in practice the meaning was equally small) makes people work longer for lesser monetary reward. He describes an experiment where people work longer making lego-figures that are kept at least until the experiment ends compared to those that must watch their last figure being demolished whilst building the new one (sisyfos variant of the experiment). In the experiment they got paid 30 cents less for each figure and built 13 figures on avarage in the first experiment compared to only 9 in the sisyfos variant before having the feeling it was ”not worth it” anymore. Consider to use this finding and relate it to political suggestion for a future monetary economy using ”meaningfulness” as the core incitements for work?
