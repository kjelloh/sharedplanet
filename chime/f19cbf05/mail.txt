2) Consider for humor to be funny it has to convey a hidden (unexpected) truth about some frightening, chaotic and destructive event or situation?

	==> Consider this is an alternative way to state the definition of humor that Henrik Schyffert proposes (https://youtu.be/KbB6dk9F7TA?t=1636 <https://youtu.be/KbB6dk9F7TA?t=1636> )?

“The task of the comedian is to provide more views on a topic that the common man has. To grab the audience by the hand. Lead them up to the terrible subject. Guide them to view the subject from all these thousands of angels. Provide some silly theory or thesis about it. And then lead them back to safety and relief and let go of the hand again."

	==> Consider we can also state this as the task of the comedian is to dig out some hidden truth about the terrible event or situation so that the audience can internalise it and possibly apply it to more efficiently relate to these kinds of events or act in such a way so that they do not occur?

	==> Consider how humor being a way for comedians to communicate truth about terrible and threatening things correlates well with Jordan B Petersson thesis that the purpose of memory is to learn from past events and when we have extracted the truth we can let go of the haunting memory?
