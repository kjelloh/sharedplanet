Could it be that ”Attentive” is the target state of the Super Curious. She does not want to become enlightened, she wants to be attentive?

1) Becoming enlightened is goal oriented and postpones the wanted life into the future. Being attentive you can start being today, in the now.
2) To become enlightened you must find the truth which we have a hard time knowing what it is. Being attentive means we can be alert in the moment and in where we are going.
3) With the goal of becoming enlightened we create barriers between different ”truths”. This separates people. By being attentive we hear what others say and are able to cooperate.
4) Being attentive is being conscious. It seems consciousness is about choosing where our attention should be. What to pay attention is what we use our free will for. We do not use our fre will to act. We use our free will to pay attention to what seems true and thus become stimulated to act-in-faith.
5) Our System 2 relies on System 1 to provide truths. Thus it is need our consciousness that is enlightened. It is our sub-consciousness that identifies nodes in out truth-webs. System 2 (our consciousness) the decides what needs attention and thus what ”truths” System 1 should process. This we call reasoning.
