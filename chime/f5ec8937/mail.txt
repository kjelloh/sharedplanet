What can we say about the Myrehearsal app in regards to what we would like such an app to be able to do (and how) and how we should design the Darwinet based Trudelutt-app?

1) Myrehearsal is account-based (account name + password login) vs Trudelutt that should be Domain invite-driven based.

	==> Consider to avoid the Multiple Digital Identity Disorder syndrome for Trudelutt?
	
2) Myrehearsal requires sound files to be downloaded and then played (two steps and different location in UI)

	==> Consider to have Trudelutt synchronise files in the Domain automatically (with graphical feed-back)
	==> Consider to have Trudelutt use a server based Node to ensure all members of the Domain to be able to synchronise at any time (e.g. when all user attended peers are offline)?

3) MyRehersal does not provide “Download All” for a role, e.g. voice like “all for baryton” (Only file by file download possible)

	==> Consider to enable Trudelutt to synchronise “views” for roles (e.g. All files for Baryton in a Domain)
	==> Consider to enable Combined-views where the user may have a view of “All Baryton Files” for “Next Rehersal meeting” or “end-of-term Consert”
		==> In this way one would be able to easily define “focus” based on several crossing aspects?

4) MyRehersal does not push nor report changes (e.g. new or changed files)

	==> Consider Trudelutt to take advantage of Darwinet evolution-pugs design to provide a view of what-has-changed, when it changed and who made the change?
	...

5) Consider to list explicit functionality for Trudelutt that Myrehearsal does not have and does not intend to have?

	a) Myrehearsal does not support creation and sharing of own recordings?
	==> Consider to have Trudelutt allow members to record and share sound files?
	==> Consider Trudelutt to detect what recordings the users actually uses (and present “favourites”)?

	b) trudelutt should have a built-in “conductor”
	==> Consider a “piano-roll” like the one used by Guitar Hero and Singalong games?
	
	c) Consider an optional piano-roll background to illustrate where in a song one is?
	==> A bond-song for example may have bond-scenes and characters turn up behind the score when different part of the song come and goes?

	d) Consider Trudelutt to allow purchase of scores in-app?
	==> E.g. using Bitcoins?

	e) Consider Trudelutt to have a built-in track mixer?
	==> Consider an Auto-mixer for rehearsal of ones own voice (having the mixer set the other voices to “nice” lower levels)?

	f) Consider Trudelutt to apply “practice levels” 100…0 where more and more info in the piano-roll is left out (e.g. text)?
	==> At 100% all text and voices are shown. At 0% only the other voices and no text is shown?

	g) Consider to enable Trudelutt to make play-lists?
	==> Perhaps Trudelutt can share play-lists into the music player on the platform?
	==> Does not seem to be allowed to export sound files into iTunes?

	…
