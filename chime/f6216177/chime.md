A Shared Planet Citizen may be defined as one who wants to maximize her deep richness and minimize her deep costs. And by Deep Cost we mean all costs in resources that matters to peoples lives (Healthy food and water, healthy air and environment, freedom and security, love and respect, connectedness). With deep richness we mean private or common ownership of resources that is of high value to a rich life (the same as are valid in deep costs).

A Shared Planet Business shall produce goods and services to the lowest deep cost as possible. A sustainable cost is the minimum. A zero cost is the best but arguably impossible way to produce anything. A fair deep cost is the goal.

A Shared Planet Citizen shall consume to get deep rich. The concepts of being poor or rich shall be defined in terms of deep richness. If one is to low in deep richness one is deeply poor. One may thus be shallow rich (have a lot of money or capital) but still be deeply poor (lack security, be deeply to costly or just plain belittled in ones potential as a human being).
