The difference between the super-Curious that aims to travel best informed and the curious that tries to get to the end station is in the way they are able to find pleasure and rest in life. The super-curious rests because he is tired or want to explore the current "place" some more. The curious rests when they arrive. If they suspect they are not "yet there" they do not want to stay. The curious keeps asking "are we there yet"? whilst the super-curious asks "what place is this?"
