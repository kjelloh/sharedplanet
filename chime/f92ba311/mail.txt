My Brother HL-L2375DW laser printer suddenly refused to print with the error "Low Toner..." but I wanted to force it to really empty the cartridge!

1) Consider to try a third party toner to see how it behaves compared to Brother own "original" (and more expensive) toner?

	==> Consider the Toner to buy is either Product TN-2410 (1200 pages) or TN-2420 (300 pages)?

	
	Brother HL-L2375DW seems to accept either 122 pages TN-2410 or 300 pages TN-2420?

	==> Consider "TBV180 inkClub Ersätter: TN-2410" third party toner for 418 SEK inc. VAT and shipping?

2) Consider what it means that it continued to print until beginning of August 2020!

So this week (around 5th of August) my Brother HL-2375DW finally gave up printing. It is quite telling that this is in fact almost 9 month after it self-activated its lock due to low toner! So this is the world we are living in now?

	==> Consider how I managed to get more prints by shaking the toner!!

I took out the toner and gave it a thorough shaking and managed to print with OK quality again! At least one page so let's see how many more pages before it really, really is empty :)

3) It seems this trick to "Reset" the Low-Toner block worked for me (https://www.inkhub.com.au/brother-tn-2450-toner-reset <https://www.inkhub.com.au/brother-tn-2450-toner-reset> )?

	==> For me it worked to select "Continue" in step 3 and then hit <Go> (and then wait around one minute!)
HL-L2375DW / HL-L2350DW /  HL L2395DW

  1. Press the Up or Down arrow key to choose General Setup, and then press OK.

  2. Press the Up or Down arrow key to choose Replace Toner, and then press OK.

  3.Press the Up or Down arrow key to choose Continue or Stop.

  4. Press Go.


	Note: I had to wait for around a minute or two for the printer to start printing again. It may or may not have been a coincident?
