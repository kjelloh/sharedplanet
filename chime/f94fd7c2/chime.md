When the cost to produce is dropping fast the use fore paid labour is going away. We get more free time but in the process gets less and less money to spend. Free time is good. Becoming poor is not.

Sooner , rather and later, do we need to address this built in Issue of the market economy paired with accelerating development.

The end station of this development could be a world where the production of all product and services we need is fully automated. No human is needed anywhere in the production cycle. We live in a fully automated crinkle economy with just one problem. No one earns anything so no one can buy anything. On the other hand, when everything is fully automated, self maintained and sustainably powered and financed (circle economy) then the price is zero. We would then again live in a hunter and gatherer society. Now with the difference that it is not they nature that automatically provides for us. We have built a virtual ”mother nature” where we can just go hunting and gathering what we need.

Now, is this a future we should strive for? If so, what happens if we have to fight for the resources? Suppose the available product and services is not enough to cover our needs. Then we would be back to survival of the fittest again?
