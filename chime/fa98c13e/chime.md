Could we imagine a way to define the organization and services of our social institutions in such a way that anyone can act as their agents?

1) Such a separation would enable social service to be circulated among all citizens? The ”what” society should do is then separated from ”whom” should do it and it would be easier to discuss them as separate entities?  
2) In Politics it could be possible to discuss goals, strategies and ideas as separate from the persons currently responsible to carry them out? Thus, e.g. being a member of riksdagen could be a duty everyone is required to perform at some period in life?  
3) The idea could be defined as if we separate the agent of a social service purpose from the person actually taking on the role of that agency?  
4) By separating personal interests from the services and purpose of society processes and institutions it should be more easy to allow experimentation of new ideas, organization and institutions? Under the paradigm of ”attention” we need to be able to create new ways of solving old and new problems instead of being fixed to have old ways solving new problems.  
5) This model would also allow computer programs to act as social service agents? Especially self-learning algoritms could be allowed to act as agents of society to reveal information and ”truths” we need to stay ”attentive” to new ways to organize and provide social services?  
6) Virtualization of societies organization and institutions could enable societies that is not tied to a geographical area, e.g. a nation? Maybe it would then be possible to have someone become a ”citizen” of a virtual ”nation” and still live in one of the spatially located nations? This could open up for a more flexible way to ”try out” new ways to define a society, its monetary system, institutions, and citizen interaction and duties?
