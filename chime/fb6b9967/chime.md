Looking for a way to "make sense" about contemporary polarisation, "culture war" and "Post Truth" one way to unite the human effort to figure out how to live together is to focus on how we "search" rather than what is "true"?

> *The idea here is to "go meta" as in finding something about what we are doing that is more important than what we actually end up doing. As humans we can argue there should be something that unite us in how we care for a good life and what it is about the good life that we care for. So to me the aspect of all the major "truth" claims from movements like atheism versus religion and left versus right is that we should suspect them to cover only some part of what we care for and that this explains why they seem to be at odds with each other? So one meta perspective about our disagreement is that we all care but we care for different things. We "value" things differently and our disagreement is what "value" is the most important. We all care. We only disagree on what we should care the most about.*

> *Now my reason to focus on how we search for truth rather than the truth we actually claim to have found may be solely due to my temperament, as in how I have turned out to care about things in life. But then I would say that this observation in itself is in fact part of caring for the search algorithm itself and not what is true. So I can say, yes, I can not claim that it is "true" that the search algorithm (how we go about to find truth) is more important than the truth we find. But I can say it is "true" that humanity will prosper more if we can find something to care together about compared to if we continue in warfare about what is the true thing to care about. Or put another way, we may be better off caring for an acceptance of difference in caring than to enforce to only be allowed to care for a set of approved caring.*

> *Ok, this all sounds very abstract. But it is in fact no more complicated than to say that we may be better off to agree on a meta-level. Where the meta perspective is to observe and identify the traits about what we are doing. So for example stating that we can model human activity to be driven by what we care for. And that this is a meta-perspective in that it is an observation about a common framing of opposing claims of what is true.*

> *Now it may be the case that to focus on the "search algorithm" may not be "meta enough" in that Sam Harris will still not agree with Christians on how to obtain truth. So in fact Sam Harris is already engaged in defining the "best" search algorithm as well as Christianity also actually defining another one. Sam Harris would build a strong case you should search for truth using the scientific method based on the "sacred ignorance" while Christianity would argue for "faith based searching" as defined by the model or reality centered on the existence of God.*
