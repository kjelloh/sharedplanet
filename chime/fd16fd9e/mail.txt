Could it be a good idea to try and define a "Grocery store of the Future” in the form of a manifest?

1) Consider to aim the Grocery Store to provide goods and services with ”small footprint” (environmental and health)?

	==> Consider to sell goods and services that has a small environmental impact (e.g. 25% less that average grocery stores)?
	==> Consider to sell goods that have a small health impact (e.g. 50% less than average grocery stores)?
	==> Consider to make the operation have a low overall ”bad" impact but hight overall ”Good” impact?
		==> Consider causing suffering as ”bad” (e.g. bad worker environments or payment in any stage of associated operations)?

2) Consider to aim at using IT foremost to create a good user experience (not foremost to reduce ”costs”)?

	==> Or Consider to include ”suffering” of the consumer as ”Cost” and aim to minimize such costs.
	==> Aim at reducing the amount of System 2 thinking and enable relying only on System 1 thinking of the customer?
		==> This should mean we must pay attention to commercial messages in the store to trigger ”beneficial” behavior (in line with the aim of the store)?

3) Consider to apply some ”cost sharing” between customers?

	==> Consider a software algorithm that adjusts the prices between customers based on the ability to pay?
	==> Consider to find a way to make the system in such a way that customers find i fare and beneficial?
	==> Consider to make the ones that pay more feel it is worth it based on gained ”pride”, ”ease of heart and mind” and time saving?
		==> I.e. it may be worth to pay more if one gains health, causes less suffering to others and get more free time to to other activities (not spend time being food)?

4) Consider to make the algorithms to measure ”suffering” be based on transparent input from experts and consumers?

	==> Customers should be able to trust the system, not have to trust the grocery store per se?
	==> Customers should be able to design their own ”profile” (what they value), share it anonymously, and use popular profiles (this build trusted communities)
	==> Consider to be clear about that ”shit will happen” and the important stuff is to learn-form-misstakes?
	==> Consider to make customer accept when things goes wrong by trusting they still do much better that if going to an ordinary grocery store?
	…

5) Consider to enable anyone to start a grocery store based on this Manifest?

	==> The ”back office” IT must be open source?
	==> Consider to use Darwinet somewhere in the IT chain?

6) Consider to enable open source systems to provide networks and processing required to create the ”suffering evaluation” back-bone?

	==> Consider to use Darwinet networks to build per-product supply-chain suffering evaluation?
	...

7) Consider to find a good name for this kind of manifest?

	==> Is it close to parts of the Shared Planet manifests?
	==> Consider ”Least Suffering Grocery Store Manifest”?
	==> Consider ”Suffer Less” Grocery Store Manifest?
	...

…

Consider to capture these ideas in a Manifest for a ”low suffering” grocery store?
