The economy-of-the-future shall carry within it incitements to develop production and consumption within the limits of sustainable resources (time, energy, materials, water). Consider to evaluate any idea of such an economy (e.g. the transaction-decay or time-decay currency) on how well it creates incitements (rewards) schemes like the WikiHouse?

1) Alastair Parvin describes the Wikihouse vision in his TED talk 2013 (http://www.ted.com/talks/alastair_parvin_architecture_for_the_people_by_the_people#t-703024)  
2) He states that banks, states or corporations will not be able to develop cities for all based on current economic models.  
3) How well does the economy-of-the-future fit with the Wikihouse scheme? How may the economy-of-the-future allow ”trading” that allows Wikihouse scheme to be prosperous?  
4) If society creates money and hands out to citizens to consume, then the price for a Wikihouse depends on the cost on our living conditions (energy, water, air, time)? If the money handed out is based on how much of the commons that are ”bought” by the producers the economy would compete to provide to consumers need within those limits?  
…

Consider to use the Wikihouse scheme as an evaluation model of any idea of the economy-of-the-future. The idea must create incitements that allow these kinds of crowd-sourced self-organizing communities to come to life to solve the problems with resource sharing we currently face.
