4) Consider the Wh requirements of the battery bank?

	==> Suppose we would like to weld for 15 minutes (continuously)?
	==> Note that each weld is normally only second up to say max a minute at a time?

	==> Maximum energy requirement for 35% duty cycle is 200A * 28V * 0,35 * 0,25 (15 minutes) = 5600W * 35% * 1/4h = 490 Wh?
	==> Maximum energy requirement for 60% duty cycle is 160A * 25V * 0,60 * 0,25 (15 minutes) = 4000W * 60% * 1/4h = 600 Wh?
	==> Maximum energy requirement for 100% duty cycle is 130A * 20,8V * 1 * 0,25 (15 minutes) = 2704W * 100% * 1/4h = 676 Wh?

	==> So, a Lead Pack of 3 x 12V * X Ah = 700 Wh ==> 3 x  Cell of 12V, 19,5 Ah (36V, 19,5 Ah)

	==> So, A Li-Ion pack of 9 x 3.7V * Y Ah =  700 Wh ==> 9 x Cell of 3.7V, 21 Ah (33V, 21 Ah)

		==> Using Sony 18650 Cells with 3 Ah capacity we need 7 cells in parallel = 21 Ah ==> 9 x 7 x Cell of 3,7V, 3 Ah (699 Wh)
