I need to make myself take the action required for me to get where I want (as in move out of my apartment into a living suitable for my creative needs)

The idea is that I somehow got the intuition that I was being watched by my future selves. I suppose the idea came from Jordan B. Petersson talks where he mentions these kind of imaginary games. Like imagining you are someone you care for. And that you can model yourself as distributed through time. And that all these instances of you, anywhere on the time axis of your life, actually both care about what you do. But also are affected by what you do!

From that I could feel how I in a sense could imagine I was being watched by both my past and my future self! ANd that this made me more aware of what I actually should be doing know from the perspective of my other past and future selves.

And we can expand on this and have past and future friends and family also "care" about what I do today. In fact, in the web of social interconnections this never ends so in some small part almost everyone that has ever lived and almost all that will ever live actually cares about what I do here and now!

I like this idea. It is something about modelling myself as an actor in this context that spans both short and vast amounts of time! And it made me think that I should try to play with this type of role play to see if it will make me take better actions today!

Also, with this model of me and all affected by me or caring about me making up a big mesh of interactions that spans over time can be thought of as an all-seeing caring external entity. In almost all aspects a model of God! So if I act as if all past, present and future me and those caring for me are watching and caring about what I do, then this is in all respects exactly the same thing as if I act as if God exists!

So far (this afternoon) I meditated and had imaginary conversation with myself in three months on a ski weekend (Ramundberget), Myself living in my next house, Myself having just moved out of my apartment and finally with my Grandma  that died 1978.
